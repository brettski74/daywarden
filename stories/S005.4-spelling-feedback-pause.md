# User Voice

As a child doing the spelling quiz, I want the next word to wait until the "correct" or "wrong" sound has finished, so that I can hear the word instead of a cheer or a fanfare playing over it.

# Use Cases

- After any answer, whether it is correct, incorrect, or a timeout, the feedback sound plays and the feedback stays on screen for 4 seconds before the next word is asked.
- The bundled "correct" sounds run for up to about 4.4 seconds and are often louder than the word recordings. The old pause of 1 second after a correct answer and 2.5 seconds after a wrong one let the next word start under the sound.
- The arithmetic and skip-counting quizzes keep their own pauses. They play no word audio, so nothing gets drowned out.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [x] After a correct answer, the next word starts playing no sooner than 4 seconds after the feedback sound starts.
- [x] The same holds after an incorrect answer and after a timeout.
- [x] When the answer passes the attempt, the end screen (or the missed-words screen) appears after the same 4-second pause.
- [x] An automated test runs the real page script on a fake clock and checks the gap between the feedback sound and the next word for all three outcomes.

# Implementation

## Files Added/Changed/Removed

- Changed `policy/spelling_page.py`: `CORRECT_PAUSE_MS` (1000) and `WRONG_PAUSE_MS` (2500) are replaced by a single `FEEDBACK_PAUSE_MS` of 4000. The page config passes it as `feedbackPause`, and the script waits that long after every answer.
- Added `tests/page_harness.js`, a Node script that runs a quiz page's script with a stub DOM, audio, and `fetch` on a fake clock, and reports when each request and each clip happened.
- Changed `tests/test_spelling.py` to add `test_the_next_word_waits_four_seconds_after_the_feedback_sound`, parametrised over `correct`, `incorrect`, and `timeout`. It feeds the harness real replies from the spelling service and is skipped when `node` is not installed.

## Change Summary

The page used to choose between a 1-second pause after a correct answer and 2.5 seconds after a wrong one before asking the next word. Most of the "correct" sounds are longer than 1 second, so the next word played under them. Every outcome now waits 4 seconds.

There was no way to test the page's timing before. The new harness runs the script exactly as the server renders it, presses Start, lets the word clip end, types an answer, presses Enter, and advances the fake clock. The test checks that the next word's clip starts at least 4 seconds after the feedback sound and that the page asks for exactly one more question. Against the old code it fails with gaps of 1 second and 2.5 seconds.

Two bundled "correct" sounds are slightly longer than 4 seconds: `success-trumpet-fanfare.mp3` (4.4 s) and `small-audience-cheer.mp3` (4.1 s). With the network round trip, their last few hundred milliseconds can still overlap the start of the next word.

## Suggested Acceptance Testing

### Acceptance test cases for S005.4

1. Rebuild with `make up` and open a spelling task. Spell several words correctly in a row until you hear the trumpet fanfare and the audience cheer. Confirm you can hear the next word clearly after each sound.
2. Spell a word wrong while the current target is still short. Confirm "Not quite" shows for about 4 seconds while the "incorrect" sound plays, then the next word plays.
3. Let the timer run out on a word. Confirm "Time ran out" shows with the bell for about 4 seconds before the next word.
4. Once the current target is met, miss a word that will not come back. Confirm its spelling stays on screen for about 4 seconds.
5. Answer the last word needed to pass. Confirm the missed-words screen (or "Well done") appears about 4 seconds later, after the sound has finished.
6. Open an arithmetic quiz and confirm it still moves on after about 1 second for a correct answer and 2.5 seconds for a wrong one.
