Title: Cut a transfer past grace

# User Voice

As a parent, I want a download that started just inside the limit to stop once it has used the grace, so that one long stream cannot run for hours past the quota.

# Use Cases

- New transfers are already refused at the limit, without grace. This story does not change that.
- An allow records the flow: the client group, the site group, the URL, the earliest time a clock assumption on that walk runs out, and a volume ceiling for each `volume_remaining` check whose true branch was taken.
- A window that was evaluated contributes the moment it next flips, plus the household `time_grace`. A true `tasks_required` contributes the next local midnight plus that same grace. A true `time_remaining` contributes the bucket end at which ended time reaches that quota's limit plus that quota's own `time_grace`. The record keeps the earliest of those times.
- A quota the transfer merely accrues to does not cut it. A limit of zero is not a deadline and not a ceiling.
- An observation re-walks the access rules only when the clock has reached that time, or a recorded volume ceiling has been reached. The re-walk uses the groups from the approval. Allow replaces the time and the ceilings. Deny or redirect cuts.
- A failed observation, a timeout, an empty body, a report with no recorded allow, or any response that is not the cut instruction leaves the transfer running.
- A policy edit does not affect a transfer until that moment. Changing client-group or site-group membership does not reclassify it. Forcing every live transfer to walk again is left for later. A household day that starts at a clock other than midnight is S009.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [x] A forwarded flow is cut when a clock deadline or a volume ceiling recorded at its allow has been reached, and walking entry again with the frozen groups no longer allows it. Time is ended buckets. Volume is stored bytes, including the open bucket.
- [x] The recorded time is the earliest of: a window condition that was evaluated, plus the household `time_grace`; a true `tasks_required`, at the next local midnight plus that grace; a true `time_remaining`, at the bucket end where ended time reaches that quota's limit plus its own `time_grace`.
- [x] A true `volume_remaining` records that quota and the byte balance at its limit plus its volume grace. A quota the flow only accrues to does not. One recorded ceiling can cut the flow while another quota it accrues to is still inside grace.
- [x] When the re-walk still allows, the deadline and the ceilings are replaced and the transfer is not cut.
- [x] An allow that did not depend on a remaining check, a window, or a task count is not cut, even when a quota it accrues to is past a positive limit and its grace.
- [x] The household defaults are five minutes and `1M` unless the file or the quota sets them. The quota's value wins for that quota. The household `time_grace` is what a window or a task count adds. An explicit zero cuts at the limit. A limit of zero does not.
- [x] A flow inside its recorded deadline and under every recorded ceiling is not cut.
- [x] No recorded allow, a stopped observation service, or a response that is not the cut instruction does not cut the flow and does not change the access decision already taken.
- [x] A new request at or past the limit is still denied by the access rules, with no dependence on this cut.

## Change Summary

Walking an allow notes when each clock assumption ends, and keeps the earliest. A window contributes its next flip plus the household `time_grace`, whether the window was true or false. A true `tasks_required` contributes the next midnight plus that grace. A true `time_remaining` contributes the bucket end at which ended time reaches that quota's limit plus its own `time_grace`. A true `volume_remaining` contributes the quota and the byte balance at the limit plus its volume grace. A false time or volume check does not. The decision document is unchanged.

The decisions service stores that record under the flow id the proxy sends, in the shared database. The observations service files the report as before. It re-walks entry only when the clock has reached the stored time or a stored ceiling has been reached, using the groups from the approval. Allow replaces the record. Deny or redirect answers `{"action":"cut"}`. No flow id, and no stored allow, stay an empty 204. The TOML ledger does too.

The addon sends the flow id on the decision and on each observation. Only the cut instruction stops a live transfer. The report sent as the flow ends can still say cut, and the addon does not tear down a transfer that has already finished.
