Title: Cut a transfer past grace
Status: Draft
Story Points: 5
Last Updated: 2026-10-26 00:49:00
---

# User Voice

As a parent, I want a download that started just inside the limit to stop once it has used the grace, so that one long stream cannot run for hours past the quota.

# Use Cases

- New transfers are already refused at the limit, without grace. This story does not change that.
- An in-progress transfer may continue while every quota it accrues to is under that quota's limit plus grace.
- When an observation shows a quota at or past its limit plus grace, the observation response tells the proxy to cut that transfer.
- A failed observation, a timeout, an empty body, or any response that is not the cut instruction leaves the transfer running.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A forwarded flow that accrues to a quota and crosses that quota's time grace or volume grace is cut. Time grace is ended-bucket time. Volume grace is stored bytes, including the open bucket.
- [ ] The household defaults are five minutes and `1M` unless the file or the quota sets them. The quota's value wins. An explicit zero cuts at the limit.
- [ ] Crossing one quota's grace cuts the flow even if another quota the flow accrues to is still inside its grace.
- [ ] A flow that is inside every applicable grace is not cut.
- [ ] Stopping the observation service, or returning a response that is not the cut instruction, does not cut the flow and does not change the access decision already taken.
- [ ] A new request at or past the limit is still denied by the access rules, with no dependence on this cut.

# Notes

Depends on S008.3 and S000.2. Not required for S008.1 through S008.5. The addon currently discards the observation response. This story is the one that reads it. The thresholds are specified in `docs/hierarchical-policy.md`.
