# User Voice

As a parent, I want to see which steps and directions of skip counting he finds hard, such as counting back by 25, and
to read any one attempt sequence by sequence, so that I know what to practise with him.

# Behaviour

## Breakdown page

- [ ] A breakdown page for one client group, period, and filters, reached from a skip counting task's trend page,
      shows for each difference, such as 5 or -25, how many sequences were asked, right on the first try, and missed.
- [ ] Choosing a difference lists its sequences, each opening its attempt.

## Skip counting attempt page

- [ ] The attempt page shows a summary of its last run, each sequence with its blanks, every try's entries, the
      outcome and points, and its expired runs. The figures match S035.1.
