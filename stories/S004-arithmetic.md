# User Voice

As a parent, I want him to practice addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division until he can answer them quickly from memory, and I want a finished session to count as one revision task.

# Use Cases

- One session shell draws weighted operands, shows a large question, times the answer, and gives immediate feedback with a sound.
- Addition, multiplication, subtraction, and division are the same shell with a different question on screen.
- The session counts as a successful revision when he reaches the configured number of correct answers. He cannot fail it by getting answers wrong. He can abandon it.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] S004.1 through S004.5 are done.
- [ ] A completed arithmetic attempt increments the daily success count from S003.1.
- [ ] The parent attempt view shows each question, the answer he gave, and whether it was correct, incorrect, or timed out.
