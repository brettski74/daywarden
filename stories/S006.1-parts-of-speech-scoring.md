# User Voice

As a parent, I want each sentence scored in a way I can explain to him: full marks when every word is in the right place, half marks when what he placed is right but incomplete, and nothing when any word is in the wrong place.

# Use Cases

- A task lists sentences. Each sentence names the parts of speech to show, and the words that belong to each one. A word that appears twice is two occurrences, matched by position, not by spelling alone.
- Sentences are presented in a random order.
- The child places some occurrences into parts of speech. Placement is stored as occurrence to category.
- A sentence scores 1 point when every expected occurrence is in the correct category and no occurrence is in a wrong category.
- A sentence scores 0.5 points when no occurrence is in a wrong category, at least one expected category exists, and every category that has an expected word contains at least one correctly placed word, but the 1-point condition was not met.
- Any other result, including one word in the wrong category or a category left empty while another is filled, scores 0.
- The attempt passes when every sentence has been submitted and the sum of sentence scores is at least the configured pass mark. Otherwise it fails.
- The task uses the pass cooldown and the optional failure cooldown from S003.1. The problem's example is a one-day failure cooldown and a 21-day pass cooldown. Those values are configuration, not constants.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The sample sentence "The quick, brown fox jumped over the lazy dog." with nouns fox and dog, verb jumped, and adjectives quick, brown, and lazy can be stored and scored.
- [ ] All of those words placed correctly scores 1. Punctuation is not part of the word the child places.
- [ ] Every category has at least one correct word, nothing is in a wrong category, and at least one expected word is unplaced: the sentence scores 0.5.
- [ ] One word in the wrong category scores 0, even if everything else is right.
- [ ] Two occurrences of the same spelling can be placed independently.
- [ ] A quiz of several sentences does not pass until all of them are submitted.
- [ ] A total below the pass mark stores outcome `failure` and does not increment the daily pass count.
- [ ] A total at or above the pass mark stores outcome `pass` and increments the daily pass count.
- [ ] After failure, eligibility follows the failure cooldown. After a pass, eligibility follows the pass cooldown.
- [ ] The parent review shows each sentence, where he placed each word, the expected placement, and the sentence score.
