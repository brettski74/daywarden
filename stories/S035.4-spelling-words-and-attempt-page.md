# User Voice

As a parent, I want to see which spelling words he gets right first time, which he keeps missing and how he misspells
them, and how this week's words improve from one task to the next, and to read any one spelling attempt word by word,
so that I can practise the hard words with him.

# Scenarios

## A word that will not stick

From the spelling task's trend page, the parent opens the words page and follows This week's words.
The tiles show 12 words asked, 71% right first time, and 4 words missed. The grid shows `necessary` missed on Monday
and Tuesday and right after a miss on Wednesday, while the other words turned to right first time by Tuesday. The list
puts `necessary` first, with Run 0 and the highest weight. Choosing it shows `necesary ×3` and `neccessary ×1`. The
parent opens Wednesday's attempt and sees he replayed the word twice before getting it right on its second ask.

# Behaviour

## Words page

A **word** is a spelling entry key, folded to lower case, within a client group, as in S033. A word is **asked** in an
attempt when at least one of its questions has an outcome. In that attempt it is **right first time** when its first
such question was correct, **right after a miss** when it was missed and later correct, and **missed** when it was
never correct. A timeout is a miss.

A word's **pool now** is Current, Revision, or Attic when its recording is, at the moment the page is shown, in the
directory that one of the chosen tasks names as its `current_dir`, `revision_dir`, or `attic_dir`, and None when it
is in none of them. It says nothing about the pool the word was in when it was asked.

- [ ] `/parent/attempts/words` shows the spelling words of the chosen client group's spelling attempts in the period
      that pass the filters. It has the Period, Set, and Outcome controls from S035.1 in its address.
- [ ] Every question with an outcome counts, including questions in expired runs, in a run left for practice mode, and
      in abandoned, unfinished, and in-progress attempts. Practice mode's walk counts in nothing.
- [ ] A **Task** filter lists the spelling tasks assigned to the client group in the policy, and any other spelling
      task it has attempts at in the period, marked "not in the policy". It starts with every task chosen and is in the
      address. Only the chosen tasks' attempts count, and only their directories decide pool now.
      *Changed in implementation:* a spelling task that is in the policy but assigned only to other client groups, and
      has attempts by this group in the period, is listed without the mark and its directories decide pool now.
- [ ] A **Pool** filter (Current, Revision, Attic, None) starts with everything chosen, is in the address, and keeps
      only the words whose pool now is chosen, in the tiles, the grid, and the list. With `spelling-younger` the only
      task chosen and only Current chosen, the words shown are those in `spelling-younger`'s `current_dir` now.
- [ ] Every ask of a kept word in the period counts, whatever pool it was asked from. A word in Current this week
      that was asked as a revision word three weeks ago is shown with only Current chosen, and Past month includes
      those earlier asks. A word in Revision now that was a current word last week is not shown.
- [ ] A **This week's words** link chooses only Current in the Pool filter, keeps the Task filter, and sets the period
      to Custom, from the day of the first attempt whose run asked exactly the words now in its task's current pool, to
      today. With two such tasks chosen, the earlier of their days is used. A word brought back into Current this week
      then shows only this week's asks, and widening the period shows its earlier ones. With no such attempt yet, the
      period is today.
- [ ] Tiles above the grid show Words asked, Right first time (the share of the words' asks that were right first
      time, as a whole percentage), Words missed (missed or right after a miss in at least one attempt), and Settled
      (words with a Run of 5 or more now).
- [ ] A spelling task's trend page from S035.1 links to the words page with only that task chosen, keeping the client
      group, period, and filters.
- [ ] With no words to show, the page says, for example, "No spelling words for younger in this period."

## Words list

- [ ] The list has one row for each word, with Word, Pool now, Asked (attempts that
      asked it), Right first time, Right after a miss, Missed, Missed % (attempts not right first time, as a share of
      Asked), Timeouts (timed-out questions), Median time (of right answers, in seconds to one decimal place), Replays
      per ask (word replays and sentence plays over its questions, to one decimal place), and Last missed (the household
      day). A homograph shows its key, such as `read.1`.
- [ ] It adds S033's all-time Run and Weight, headed as figures for now, which match `bin/word-stats` for that client
      group whatever the period and filters.
- [ ] It is sorted by Missed %, then Asked, largest first. Each column header sorts by that column, and the sort is in
      the address.
- [ ] At phone width the list scrolls sideways inside its own box, and the page does not scroll sideways.

## Word grid

- [ ] A grid above the list has a row for each word, in the list's order, and a column for each attempt that asked any
      of the words, oldest on the left, headed with its start date.
- [ ] Each cell shows whether the word was right first time, right after a miss, missed, or not asked in that attempt,
      with an icon as well as colour, and a key explains them.
- [ ] Pointing at a cell, or focusing it, shows a readout with the word, the attempt's start date and time, and his
      answers each time, such as `necesary, necessary` or `timeout, necessary`. The arrow keys move between cells.
- [ ] Clicking a cell that was asked, or Enter on it, opens that attempt's page. A row's word chooses that word, as in
      the list.
- [ ] At phone width the grid scrolls inside its own box, and the page does not scroll sideways.

## Chosen word

- [ ] Clicking a word in the list or the grid, or Enter on it, chooses it, and the choice is in the address. Below the
      list, the chosen word shows each different misspelling he typed with how many times, most first, such as
      `necesary ×3, neccessary ×1`, and the timeouts.
      *Changed in implementation:* misspellings are folded to lower case, as the quiz judges them, so `Necesary` and
      `necesary` count as one.
- [ ] Below that, the attempts that asked it are listed newest first, each with its start date, task, the pool it was
      asked from, and his answers each time. Each opens that attempt's page.

## Spelling attempt page

- [ ] A spelling attempt's page shows a summary of its last run: current points against current required, revision
      points against revision required, such as "8 / 8 current, 3 / 4 revision", Questions, Wrong, Timeouts, Time
      answering, and Average per right answer. Each matches the attempt's figures in S035.1.
- [ ] A table lists the run's questions in order. Each row shows the word, the pool it was asked from, his answer, the
      outcome, whether the word came back to be asked again, word replays, sentence plays, the time taken, and the time
      allowed. A wrong answer and a timeout are marked with an icon and a word as well as colour.
- [ ] Each word links to the words page with that word chosen, for the attempt's client group and Past month.
- [ ] Each earlier run is listed after the last run, under a heading saying it expired or was left for practice mode,
      with the time, and with the same summary and table. A run started by practice mode lists the words practised
      before it. Earlier runs do not count toward the last run's summary.
- [ ] An attempt in progress shows the summary and the questions so far. The "Given up and carried on" section and the
      Stored record section work as in S035.2.

## MCP

- [ ] A read-only `get_spelling_words` tool (S036.1) takes a client group, the attempts filters, and the Task and Pool
      filters, and gives the tiles and each word's list figures, as the words page shows them. Naming a word adds its
      misspellings and the attempts that asked it.
- [ ] `get_attempt` gives a spelling attempt's runs, as its page shows them.

# Notes

Depends on S035.1 for the controls, the figures, and the trend page, S033 for Run and Weight, and S036.1 for the MCP
server. Follows S035.2's facts page and attempt page, and should reuse its sortable list, chosen-item address, Fold,
and FactGrid's keyboard movement where they fit.

Words, misspellings, and the grid are worked out from each attempt's stored questions when the page is asked for, like
S035.2's facts. Pool now is read from the chosen tasks' directories when the page is asked for, as
`bin/word-stats --task` does. S023.2 refuses a word in two of one task's pools, but with two tasks chosen a word can be
in Current for one and Revision for the other, by shared or separate directories. It then shows both as its pool now
and matches either choice. A task not in the policy has no directories, so its words are None unless another chosen
task holds them.

The figures are counted per attempt, as S033 counts them, because a missed word is put back on the queue and asked
again in the same attempt. Counting questions would make a word he missed once look asked twice. Timeouts are the one
column counted per question.

The grid can get wide over a long period, so it scrolls in its own box. Grouping its columns by day is left for later,
as S035's notes leave combining attempts on the trend.

This week's words finds the day the list began from the current words stored on each run, so it needs no record of
when Replace Current was used.

Out of scope: a filter on the pool a word was asked from (each stored question records it, so it can be added later
if wanted), marking the letters of a misspelling that differ from the right spelling, a page of its own for each word,
playing a word's recording from the words page, and words in a pool that were not asked in the period.

Sized against S035.2 (13 points), which had the same parts: a page of figures across attempts with a grid, a sorted
list, a chosen item, and an attempt page with runs. This story adds the Task and Pool filters read from the
policy and the data directory, This week's words, the tiles, a second kind of grid, and the MCP tool, which add up to
about 8 more.

## Change Summary

Words are worked out from each stored question when the page is asked for, like S035.2's facts. The data endpoint
takes the period, the Set and Outcome filters, and the Task and Pool filters. The chosen word and the sort are the
page's own, so choosing a word or sorting does not ask for the data again. Each word carries the attempts that asked
it, with the result and his answers, and the grid is drawn from those, so neither needs a second request. The Pool
filter's value for None is `no_pool` in the address, because `none` already means nothing chosen there.

Pool now is read with the same directory reading `bin/word-stats --task` uses. Run and Weight come from S033's ledger
for the whole client group, so they match `bin/word-stats` whatever the period. The Word column shows the entry key as
it was last stored, so a capital in a key is kept. Last missed is the household day the missed question was issued.
Replays per ask is word replays plus sentence plays over the word's questions with an outcome.

This week's words looks through all of each chosen task's attempts, whatever the period and filters, for the first
run whose stored current words are exactly the task's current pool now, folded to lower case. A task with an empty
current pool is skipped.

Without a word named, `get_spelling_words` leaves out each word's misspellings and attempts and the grid's columns. With
one, it adds them under `word`, each attempt with its start and task.

The pages were checked in jsdom only. Chrome was not connected, so the layout, the grid's colours, and the scrolling at
phone width have not been checked in a browser.

## Suggested Acceptance Testing

### Acceptance test cases for S035.4

Rebuild and start with `make up`, and sign in to the parent pages. The checks need `younger` to have a spelling task
such as `spelling-younger` with a current pool of about 10 words and a revision pool, with a week of attempts that
miss a word such as `necessary` in different ways, a run that expired, a run left for practice mode, an abandoned
attempt, and one in progress.

1. **A word that will not stick.** Follow the scenario from the task's trend page through This week's words to
   Wednesday's attempt. Check the tiles against the list. Covers the trend link, This week's words, the tiles, and the
   scenario.
2. **The list.** Check each column against the attempt pages, the default order, each header's sort, and a homograph
   such as `read.1`. Compare Run and Weight with `bin/word-stats --client younger` under two different periods. Reload
   after each choice. Covers the Words list area.
3. **The grid.** Check the columns and their dates, the icons and the key, the readout by pointer and by Tab and the
   arrow keys, Enter and a click on an asked cell, and a row's word. Covers the Word grid area.
4. **Chosen word.** Choose a word from the grid, the list, and an attempt page's link. Check the misspellings, the
   timeouts, and the attempts with the pool each asked from. Covers the Chosen word area.
5. **Filters and what counts.** Move a word from Current to Revision and another to the attic with Task
   Administration, and add a second spelling task sharing the revision directory. Try each Pool choice, each Task
   choice, Past month against Past week, and a task name removed from the policy. Compare counts with the expired run,
   the run left for practice, and the abandoned and in-progress attempts. Open a period with nothing in it. Covers the
   Words page checks on counting, the Task and Pool filters, and the empty message.
6. **Phone width.** Open the words page in a narrow window or the browser's phone view over Past month. The grid and
   the list scroll in their own boxes and the page does not scroll sideways. Covers both phone width checks.
7. **Spelling attempt page.** Open an attempt with an expired run and a run left for practice mode, and one in
   progress. Check the summaries against the trend's readout, the questions table, the marks, the word links, the
   headings, the practised words, Given up and carried on, and Stored record. Covers the Spelling attempt page area.
8. **MCP.** With an agent connected, call `get_spelling_words` with and without `word`, `task`, and `pool`, and
   `get_attempt` on a spelling attempt, and compare them with the pages. Sign in as a reviewer and open the words page.
   Covers the MCP area.
