# User Voice

As a parent, I want to see in the portal which words he spells well and which he keeps missing, and to read any one
spelling attempt word by word, so that I can practise the hard words with him.

# Behaviour

## Words page

- [ ] A words page shows S033's figures (attempts, correct, incorrect, run, and weight) for one client group, reached
      from a spelling task's trend page, with filters for a task's pools as `bin/word-stats` has.
- [ ] Choosing a word lists the attempts that asked it, with each spelling he typed, each opening its attempt.

## Spelling attempt page

- [ ] The attempt page shows a summary of its last run, each word asked with its pool, his answer, the outcome,
      replays, and times, and its earlier runs, including practice. The figures match S035.1.
