# User Voice

As a parent, I want to see which spelling words he gets right first time, which he keeps missing and how he misspells
them, and how this week's words improve from one task to the next, and to read any one spelling attempt word by word,
so that I can practise the hard words with him.

# Scenarios

## A word that will not stick

From the spelling task's trend page, the parent opens the words page for Past week with the Pool filter on Current.
The tiles show 12 words asked, 71% right first time, and 4 words missed. The grid shows `necessary` missed on Monday
and Tuesday and right after a miss on Wednesday, while the other words turned to right first time by Tuesday. The list
puts `necessary` first, with Run 0 and the highest weight. Choosing it shows `necesary ×3` and `neccessary ×1`. The
parent opens Wednesday's attempt and sees he replayed the word twice before getting it right on its second ask.

# Behaviour

## Words page

A **word** is a spelling entry key, folded to lower case, within a client group, as in S033. A word is **asked** in an
attempt when at least one of its questions has an outcome. In that attempt it is **right first time** when its first
such question was correct, **right after a miss** when it was missed and later correct, and **missed** when it was
never correct. A timeout is a miss. A word's **pool now** is the pool it is in now, across the spelling tasks in the
policy: Current, Revision, Attic, or None when it is in none of them.

- [ ] `/parent/attempts/words` shows the spelling words of every spelling attempt for the chosen client group in the
      period that passes the filters, whichever spelling task the attempt belongs to. It has the Period, Set, and
      Outcome controls from S035.1 in its address.
- [ ] Every question with an outcome counts, including questions in expired runs, in a run left for practice mode, and
      in abandoned, unfinished, and in-progress attempts. Practice mode's walk counts in nothing.
- [ ] A Pool filter (Current, Revision, Attic, None) starts with everything chosen, is in the address, and keeps only
      the words whose pool now is chosen, in the tiles, the grid, and the list. A word in the current pool of one task
      and the revision pool of another shows both and matches either.
- [ ] Tiles above the grid show Words asked, Right first time (the share of the words' asks that were right first
      time, as a whole percentage), Words missed (missed or right after a miss in at least one attempt), and Settled
      (words with a Run of 5 or more now).
- [ ] A spelling task's trend page from S035.1 links to the words page, keeping the client group, period, and filters.
- [ ] With no words to show, the page says, for example, "No spelling words for younger in this period."

## Words list

- [ ] The list has one row for each word, with Word, Pool now, Asked (attempts that asked it), Right first time, Right
      after a miss, Missed, Missed % (attempts not right first time, as a share of Asked), Timeouts (timed-out questions),
      Median time (of right answers, in seconds to one decimal place), Replays per ask (word replays and sentence plays
      over its questions, to one decimal place), and Last missed (the household day). A homograph shows its key, such
      as `read.1`.
- [ ] It adds S033's all-time Run and Weight, headed as figures for now, which match `bin/word-stats` for that client
      group whatever the period and filters.
- [ ] It is sorted by Missed %, then Asked, largest first. Each column header sorts by that column, and the sort is in
      the address.
- [ ] At phone width the list scrolls sideways inside its own box, and the page does not scroll sideways.

## Word grid

- [ ] A grid above the list has a row for each word, in the list's order, and a column for each attempt that asked any
      of the words, oldest on the left, headed with its start date.
- [ ] Each cell shows whether the word was right first time, right after a miss, missed, or not asked in that attempt,
      with an icon as well as colour, and a key explains them.
- [ ] Pointing at a cell, or focusing it, shows a readout with the word, the attempt's start date and time, and his
      answers each time, such as `necesary, necessary` or `timeout, necessary`. The arrow keys move between cells.
- [ ] Clicking a cell that was asked, or Enter on it, opens that attempt's page. A row's word chooses that word, as in
      the list.
- [ ] At phone width the grid scrolls inside its own box, and the page does not scroll sideways.

## Chosen word

- [ ] Clicking a word in the list or the grid, or Enter on it, chooses it, and the choice is in the address. Below the
      list, the chosen word shows each different misspelling he typed with how many times, most first, such as
      `necesary ×3, neccessary ×1`, and the timeouts.
- [ ] Below that, the attempts that asked it are listed newest first, each with its start date, task, the pool it was
      asked from, and his answers each time. Each opens that attempt's page.

## Spelling attempt page

- [ ] A spelling attempt's page shows a summary of its last run: current points against current required, revision
      points against revision required, such as "8 / 8 current, 3 / 4 revision", Questions, Wrong, Timeouts, Time
      answering, and Average per right answer. Each matches the attempt's figures in S035.1.
- [ ] A table lists the run's questions in order. Each row shows the word, the pool it was asked from, his answer, the
      outcome, whether the word came back to be asked again, word replays, sentence plays, the time taken, and the time
      allowed. A wrong answer and a timeout are marked with an icon and a word as well as colour.
- [ ] Each word links to the words page with that word chosen, for the attempt's client group and Past month.
- [ ] Each earlier run is listed after the last run, under a heading saying it expired or was left for practice mode,
      with the time, and with the same summary and table. A run started by practice mode lists the words practised
      before it. Earlier runs do not count toward the last run's summary.
- [ ] An attempt in progress shows the summary and the questions so far. The "Given up and carried on" section and the
      Stored record section work as in S035.2.

## MCP

- [ ] A read-only `get_spelling_words` tool (S036.1) takes a client group, the attempts filters, and the Pool filter,
      and gives the tiles and each word's list figures, as the words page shows them. Naming a word adds its
      misspellings and the attempts that asked it.
- [ ] `get_attempt` gives a spelling attempt's runs, as its page shows them.

# Notes

Depends on S035.1 for the controls, the figures, and the trend page, S033 for Run and Weight, and S036.1 for the MCP
server. Follows S035.2's facts page and attempt page, and should reuse its sortable list, chosen-item address, Fold,
and FactGrid's keyboard movement where they fit.

Words, misspellings, and the grid are worked out from each attempt's stored questions when the page is asked for, like
S035.2's facts. Pool now is read from the spelling tasks' directories when the page is asked for, as
`bin/word-stats --pool` does. The words page covers every spelling task for a client group, so it has no task filter.
Revisit if a household uses several spelling tasks with different pools for one client group.

The figures are counted per attempt, as S033 counts them, because a missed word is put back on the queue and asked
again in the same attempt. Counting questions would make a word he missed once look asked twice. Timeouts are the one
column counted per question.

The grid can get wide over a long period, so it scrolls in its own box. Grouping its columns by day is left for later,
as S035's notes leave combining attempts on the trend.

Out of scope: marking the letters of a misspelling that differ from the right spelling, a page of its own for each word,
playing a word's recording from the words page, and words in a pool that were not asked in the period.

Sized against S035.2 (13 points), which had the same parts: a page of figures across attempts with a grid, a sorted
list, a chosen item, and an attempt page with runs. This story adds the Pool filter read from the data directory, the
tiles, a second kind of grid, and the MCP tool, which add up to about 8 more.
