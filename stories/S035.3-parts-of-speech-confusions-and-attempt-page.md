# User Voice

As a parent, I want to see which parts of speech he confuses across his attempts, such as adverbs sorted as
adjectives, and to read any one attempt sentence by sentence and try by try, so that I can talk those parts through
with him.

# Behaviour

## Confusions page

- [ ] A confusions page for one client group, period, and filters, reached from a parts of speech task's trend page,
      counts every word sorted in the period by the part it belongs in and the part he put it in on his first try,
      with "no part" for a word that belongs in none and "left out" for a word he did not place.
- [ ] Each part shows how often its words were placed right on the first try.
- [ ] Choosing a pair of parts lists the words and sentences where it happened, each opening its attempt.

## Parts of speech attempt page

- [ ] The attempt page shows a summary of its last run, the sentences in order with each try's placement against the
      answer, and its expired runs, as S006.4 described. The figures match S035.1.
