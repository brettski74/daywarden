# User Voice

As a child, I want to hear the word and type it into a big box, and I want another chance at the ones I miss, so that I can keep going until this week's spelling is done.

# Use Cases

- Let `m` be the size of this week's pool, `n` the number of this week's words required today, and `x` the number of revision words required.
- At the start, choose `x` distinct words at random from the revision pool. Shuffle those together with every word from this week's pool.
- Play the word-only audio, then start the timer. The box is focused and empty.
- A control at the top left replays the word. A control at the top right plays the sentence. While either clip is playing, the timer pauses and the input box is disabled.
- Enter submits the spelling. Comparison ignores leading and trailing whitespace and is case-insensitive.
- A correct word scores one point toward the pool it came from and plays the correct sound.
- A wrong this-week word is added to the end of the queue only if the this-week correct count is still below `n`.
- A wrong revision word adds a different revision word to the end of the queue: one that has not already been spelled correctly in this attempt and is not already waiting in the queue. If no such word exists, the same word may be asked again.
- The attempt succeeds only when all three are true: every this-week word has been asked at least once, at least `n` this-week words are correct, and at least `x` revision words are correct.
- There is no failure outcome. Abandoning uses S003.2.
- The input box uses a large font and is wide enough for the word "extemporaneously" at that font size.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The opening queue contains every this-week word and exactly `x` distinct revision words, in an order that is not the stored order. A test with a fixed random seed asserts the chosen revision words and the shuffle.
- [ ] A correct spelling increments only the pool that word belongs to.
- [ ] While fewer than `n` this-week words are correct, a miss on a this-week word places that word at the end of the queue.
- [ ] Once `n` this-week words are correct, a miss on a remaining this-week word does not place it back on the queue. That word still counts as having been asked.
- [ ] A miss on a revision word appends a revision word that is not already correct in this attempt and not already queued, when such a word exists.
- [ ] The success count from S003.1 does not increase until all three completion conditions are met, including the case where both correct-counts were reached before the last this-week word had been asked.
- [ ] Replaying the word or playing the sentence pauses the timer, and keystrokes during playback do not change the box.
- [ ] The timeout is the configured constant for every word.
- [ ] The input box fits "extemporaneously" on one line at the quiz font size, checked in a browser.
- [ ] Each answer, the expected spelling, the pool, and whether it was correct are stored on the attempt and visible in the parent review from S003.3.
