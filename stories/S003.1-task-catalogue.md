Ticket: S003.1
Title: Task catalogue, cooldown, and attempts
Status: Draft
Story Points: 5
Last Updated: 2026-10-31 12:31:02
---

# User Voice

As a parent, I want each revision task to remember which client group it is for, when it may be used again, when it should stop being optional, and when it has been passed often enough to stay finished, so that a quiz he already knows does not come back every afternoon, a quiz he failed can come back sooner, and a quiz I need him to face cannot be put off forever.

# Use Cases

- A task has a type, a name, a body of type-specific configuration, a pass cooldown in whole household days, and an optional failure cooldown in whole household days. It may also have `available_from`, `due_date`, `max_skips`, and `max_passes`. Each of those is optional. Leaving one out leaves that rule off.
- The same type can be configured more than once. The name tells the tasks apart. `parts-of-speech-1` and `parts-of-speech-2` are two tasks. Each has its own sentences, dates, skip limit, and attempt history. A new set of work is a new task. Editing a task a client group has already passed does not ask that group to pass it again.
- `available_from` and `due_date` are household dates with day resolution, written as `2026-10-31`. They take effect at the start of that household day. The household day is local midnight unless S009 sets another boundary.
- `max_skips` is a whole number, zero or more. A household day is one skip when this client group failed or abandoned the task on that day, since the group's last pass. No pass yet means every such day counts. The day is the household day of the attempt's finish. A second failure, a second abandon, or one of each on that same day is still one skip. A pass clears the skip days, and the next time the task is eligible the count starts from zero. Skip days are derived from attempt rows whether or not `max_skips` is currently set, so turning the limit on later includes failure and abandon days already stored since the last pass.
- `max_passes` is a whole number, zero or more. The task is not eligible for a client group whose stored passes for it are already at least that number. A value of 3 means the third pass is stored and counts toward today's revision, and the task is not offered after that while the field stays at 3. A value of 0 means it is not eligible before any pass. The count is that group's pass rows for this task. Another group's passes do not retire it. Raising the number, or clearing the field, can make it eligible again, still subject to cooldown and `available_from`. Lowering it to a number the group has already reached retires it. An attempt already in progress can be finished, and a pass from it is stored and counts toward the cap.
- A task is assigned to one or more client groups. Every address in an assigned group shares that group's attempt history. An address in another group does not. Passes and skip days are per client group, so one group's pass does not clear another group's due date, skip count, or pass cap. The dates, `max_skips`, and `max_passes` sit on the task and apply to every group it is assigned to.
- An attempt records the client group, the task, the start time, the finish time, the outcome (`in_progress`, `pass`, `failure`, or `abandoned`), and a JSON blob of answers and results that the quiz type fills in.
- A task is eligible when it is assigned to that client group, `available_from` is unset or today is on or after it, `max_passes` is unset or the group has fewer stored passes than that number, and the cooldown for the last finished attempt has elapsed. The pass cooldown applies after a pass. The failure cooldown applies after failure. An abandoned attempt does not start a cooldown. No previous attempt means the cooldown check passes. A task that has reached `max_passes` is not eligible, so a due date or a skip limit does not make it required.
- A cooldown of N days means the task is eligible again on the household day that is N day-boundaries after the finish day. A cooldown of 0 means it is eligible again immediately. A cooldown of 1 means it is eligible again on the next household day. With no boundary configured, that boundary is local midnight.
- A task is required for a client group today when it is eligible for that group today and at least one of these is true:
  - `due_date` is set, today is on or after that date, and the group has no pass for the task.
  - `max_skips` is set, and the number of skip days before today is at least `max_skips`. A skip recorded today does not make the task required today.
- Either condition is enough. A due date that has not arrived does not block a skip limit, and unused skips do not postpone a due date that has arrived. A pass before the due date means the due date never makes the task required. After that pass, `max_skips` can still require the task on a later cycle, once the pass cooldown has ended and he has used the skips again.
- A task inside its cooldown is not eligible, so it is not required that day. A failure marks that household day as a skip and does not clear the due date. Another failure or abandon later the same day does not mark it again. On the next day the task is eligible, it is required again when the due date or the skip count still says so.
- `available_from`, `due_date`, `max_skips`, and `max_passes` have no effect on `parent_check`. That type is never required, and a parent may record it more than once. S003.2 says how a parent records one, and that the child is never sent to it.
- S003.2 says when a required task may be abandoned. An abandon or a failure still marks that household day as one skip, including when the task was already required. It does not mark the day twice.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A task assigned to one client group is not eligible for a different client group. Two addresses in the assigned group are both eligible, and they see the same attempt history.
- [ ] After a pass with a 21-day cooldown, the task is not eligible on day 20 and is eligible on day 21, measured on the household day.
- [ ] After a failure with a 1-day failure cooldown and a 21-day pass cooldown, the task is eligible again on the next household day.
- [ ] After an abandonment, the task stays eligible.
- [ ] An in-progress attempt for that client group and task is returned if the child comes back, rather than starting a second parallel attempt.
- [ ] Today's pass count used by `tasks_required` equals the number of attempts for that client group that reached `pass` on the current household day. One pass adds one point.
- [ ] A pass recorded at the first moment of the household day counts toward the new day. With no boundary configured, a pass at 00:10 local time counts toward the new day.
- [ ] Attempt rows survive a restart, and skip days derived from those rows do too.
- [ ] With `available_from` set to tomorrow, the task is not eligible today. On that date it is eligible when the cooldown check passes.
- [ ] With `due_date` still in the future, the task is not required, including when it has never been passed.
- [ ] On `due_date`, with no pass for this client group and the task eligible, the task is required. A different client group that has already passed the same task is not required by that date.
- [ ] A pass recorded before `due_date` means that date does not make the task required for that client group.
- [ ] A due task failed yesterday, with a 1-day failure cooldown, is not eligible today and is not required today. On the next household day it is eligible and required again. The failure is not a pass.
- [ ] Two tasks of one type, with different names and due dates, keep separate attempt histories. Passing one does not change the other.
- [ ] `max_skips` of 3 allows a skip on three household days, by failure or by abandon, without the task becoming required on any of those three days. On the next household day the task is eligible, it is required. A further failure or abandon on a day that is already a skip does not add another.
- [ ] The calendar case holds when he abandons once each day and the day boundary is local midnight. The task is added on Monday 2026-11-02 with `max_skips` 3, a 21-day pass cooldown, and no earlier attempts. He may abandon it on 2, 3, and 4 November, and it is required on 5 November. A pass on 5 November makes it eligible again on 26 November. He may abandon it on 26, 27, and 28 November, and it is required on 29 November.
- [ ] A pass clears the skip days. With `max_skips` of 3, two abandon days and a failure on a later household day are three skip days. The task is not required on the day of that failure. It is required on the next household day it is eligible.
- [ ] Three failures on the same household day, with a failure cooldown of 0, are one skip. A failure and an abandon on that same day are still one skip. With `max_skips` of 3 and no earlier skip day, the task is not required the next household day.
- [ ] With a 1-day failure cooldown and `max_skips` of 3, failures on 2, 3, and 4 November 2026 leave the task required on 5 November, the same as three abandon days.
- [ ] `max_skips` of 0 makes the task required on the first household day it is eligible.
- [ ] With both `due_date` and `max_skips` set, the task is required on the due date while skip days are still under the limit, and it is required by the skip limit while the due date is still in the future.
- [ ] With `available_from` after `due_date`, and no pass yet, the task is not offered before `available_from`. On `available_from` it is required.
- [ ] The same fields on a `parent_check` task do not make it required and do not limit how many times a parent may record one.
- [ ] With `max_passes` of 3 and two stored passes, the task is eligible again once its cooldown has elapsed. The third pass increments today's revision count. After that pass is stored, the task is not eligible on a later household day, including the day its pass cooldown would have ended.
- [ ] A different client group with fewer than three passes for that task is still eligible. Another task of the same type keeps its own pass count.
- [ ] With three stored passes, a `due_date` of today does not make the task eligible or required.
- [ ] Raising `max_passes` above the stored passes, or clearing it, makes the task eligible again once the cooldown has elapsed. Lowering it to the stored count, or below that count, removes eligibility. An attempt already in progress can still be finished, and that pass is stored.
- [ ] `max_passes` of 0 leaves the task not eligible for a group with no passes.

# Notes

Depends on S008.1 for client groups, and on S008.2 for the condition that reads the count. Quiz-specific configuration is an opaque document at this layer. S004, S005, and S006 define their own fields. Weekday targets inside a spelling task stay on that task. They are not a policy-rule override.

`due_date` is the day an unpassed task becomes required to attempt. It does not promise that he has passed by that day. A failure cooldown can push the next attempt past the date. A day he fails or gives up is one skip, and once `max_skips` such days are behind him the task is required on the next household day it is eligible. He can still fail that required attempt. That day is one more skip, and the following eligible day is required again until he passes. Reading those attempts stays with the parent. S003.3 is where they are listed.

S009 is the household day these dates, cooldowns, and skip days use. Until that boundary is configured, the day starts at local midnight.
