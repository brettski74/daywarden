# User Voice

As a parent, I want to see which sums, differences, products, and quotients he gets wrong or answers slowly across all
his arithmetic attempts, and to read any one attempt question by question, so that I know which facts to practise
with him.

# Behaviour

## Facts page

A **fact** is one question as it is asked, given by its left number `m` and right number `n`, as in the policy's
`first` and `operands`. `6 + 7` and `7 + 6` are two facts. A fact is **missed** when it is answered wrongly or times
out.

- [ ] `/parent/attempts/facts/<operation>`, for `addition`, `subtraction`, `multiplication`, or `division`, shows that
      operation's facts from every arithmetic attempt in the period that passes the filters, whichever task it
      belongs to. It has the Period, Set, and Outcome controls from S035.1 in its address, and links to the other
      three operations that keep them.
- [ ] Every question counts, including questions in expired runs and in abandoned, unfinished, and in-progress
      attempts. A fact asked twice in one attempt counts twice.
- [ ] A grid has a row for each `m` and a column for each `n` that was asked. The cell for `m` 6 and `n` 7 is
      `6 + 7`, `13 − 6`, `6 × 7`, or `42 ÷ 6`. A fact never asked has an empty cell.
- [ ] Cells are shaded by Misses, the share of asks missed, or by Time, the median time of right answers. Misses is
      the default, the choice is in the address, and a key explains the shading. Each asked cell also shows its right
      answers and asks as text, such as `3/5`, so nothing relies on colour alone, and a line above the grid says so:
      "Under each question: times right / times asked in this period, such as 3/5."
- [ ] Pointing at a cell, or focusing it, shows a readout with the question and its answer, such as `7 × 8 = 56`, and
      its asked, right, wrong, timeouts, median time of right answers, and the date it was last missed. The arrow keys
      move between cells.
- [ ] At phone width the grid scrolls inside its own box, and the page does not scroll sideways.
- [ ] A fact list under the grid has one row for each fact asked, with Fact (such as `7 × 8 = 56`), Asked, Right,
      Wrong, Timeouts, Missed (a percentage), Median time, and Last missed. It is sorted by Missed, then Asked, largest
      first. Each column header sorts by that column, and the sort is in the address.
- [ ] Clicking a cell or a list row, or Enter on it, chooses that fact, and the choice is in the address. Below the
      list, the attempts that asked it are shown newest first, each with its start date, task, and his answer each
      time, such as `54, 54, 56` or `timeout, 56`. Each opens that attempt's page.
- [ ] An arithmetic task's trend page from S035.1 links to the facts page for its operation, keeping the client group,
      period, and filters.
- [ ] With no questions to show, the page says, for example, "No multiplication questions for younger in this period."

## Arithmetic attempt page

- [ ] An arithmetic attempt's page shows a summary of its last run: the operation, points against the pass mark, such
      as "20 / 20 right", Questions, Wrong, Timeouts, Time answering, and Average per right answer. Each matches the
      attempt's figures in S035.1.
- [ ] A table lists the run's questions in order. Each row shows the question with its answer, such as
      `6 + 7 = 13`, his answer, the outcome, the time taken, and the time allowed. An empty answer is shown empty, not
      as 0.
- [ ] A wrong answer and a timeout are marked with an icon and a word as well as colour.
- [ ] Each question links to the facts page for its operation with that fact chosen, for the attempt's client group
      and Past month.
- [ ] Each expired run is listed after the last run, under a heading with the time it expired, with the same summary
      and table. It does not count toward the last run's summary.
- [ ] An attempt in progress shows the summary and the questions so far.
- [ ] The "Given up and carried on" section stays. The stored record moves into a Stored record section that starts
      closed and opens and closes like other portal sections.
- [ ] Attempts of other types show as before.

# Notes

Depends on S035.1 for the controls, the figures, and the trend page. Replaces S004.6, whose summary and table are
carried over here, with the figures defined once in S035.1.

The fact comes from each stored question's `m` and `n`, and its text from `question` and `expected`. Facts are
worked out when the page is asked for, like S035.1's figures.

The time allowed shrinks as he gets more right, so a timeout late in a run is a harder test than one early on. Median
time of right answers is used rather than timeouts alone for that reason. Comparing a fact's time with the time it
was allowed is left for later, if the median turns out not to be enough.

Grids are only as large as the numbers asked, so a single times table is one row. Addition with operands up to 20 is
a 20 × 20 grid, which is why it scrolls in its own box.

## Change Summary

Facts are worked out from each stored question when the page is asked for. A question's operation is read from the
sign in its stored text, not from the attempt's settings, because a run that starts after the task's operation changed
asks under the new one. A question still showing has no outcome yet and is not counted on the facts page. Last missed
is the household day the missed question was issued.

The data endpoint takes the period and filters only. The chosen fact, the shading, and the sort are the page's own, so
choosing a fact, sorting, or changing the shading does not ask for the data again. Each fact carries the attempts that
asked it with his answers, so the chosen fact's list needs no second request.

Misses shading has five levels: none missed, under 25%, 25% to 49%, 50% to 74%, and 75% or more. Time shading splits
the range of the facts' median times into four equal bands, and a fact with no right answers is marked "No right
answers". Each level's colours are role properties in the theme, with ivory text on the darkest level.

An expired run's pass mark is the one stored on the attempt, which is the last run's, because the record keeps no
other. The trend links to the operation of the task's latest attempt, or the policy's when it has none in store.

The grid, readout, and list were checked in jsdom, and the endpoints against a scratch service seeded with real quiz
records. Chrome was not connected, so the layout, the shading colours, and the grid's own scrolling at phone width
have not been checked in a browser.

## Suggested Acceptance Testing

### Acceptance test cases for S035.2

Rebuild and start with `make up`, and sign in to the parent pages. The checks need a client group with a few weeks of
attempts at a multiplication task and an addition task with operands up to 20, including a run that expired after an
hour idle, an abandoned attempt, a practice attempt, and one attempt in progress.

1. **Facts grid and list.** From the multiplication task's trend page, follow its facts link. Check the grid's rows and
   columns, the empty cells, the `3/5` right and asked counts, both shadings and their keys, the readout by pointer and
   by Tab and the arrow keys, and the list's columns, default order, and each header's sort. Reload after each choice.
   Covers the Facts page grid, shading, readout, list, and trend link checks.
2. **Choosing a fact.** Click a cell, Enter on another, and click a list row. Check the attempts that asked it, newest
   first, with answers such as `timeout, 56`, and that each opens its attempt. Covers the chosen fact check.
3. **What counts.** Compare a fact's counts against the attempt pages, including the expired run, the abandoned and
   in-progress attempts, and a fact asked twice in one attempt. Change Period, Set, and Outcome, and follow the links to
   the other operations. Open an operation with nothing in the period. Covers the address, counting, and empty checks.
4. **Phone width.** Open addition's facts in a narrow window or the browser's phone view. The grid scrolls in its own
   box and the page does not scroll sideways. Covers the phone width check.
5. **Arithmetic attempt page.** Open an attempt that passed after an expired run. Check the summary against the trend's
   readout, the questions table, the Wrong and Timeout marks, an empty answer, each question's link to its fact, and
   the expired run after it. Open the attempt in progress. Open and close Stored record. Open a spelling attempt and
   check it is as before. Covers the Arithmetic attempt page area.
