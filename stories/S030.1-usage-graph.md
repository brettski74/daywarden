# User Voice

As a parent, I want a line graph of usage, in minutes or in bytes, that I can filter by client group, site group, and
quota, split into one line per group, and set to a period such as the past week or the previous month, and I want to
save the settings I use often under a name, so that the comparisons I check every week are one choice away.

# Behaviour

## Pages

- [ ] Usage in the sidebar is a group that expands into Minutes and Bytes, indented beneath it. It expands, collapses,
  and remembers whether it is expanded as Task Administration does in S023.2.
- [ ] Minutes opens `/parent/usage/minutes` and Bytes opens `/parent/usage/bytes`. Both have the same filters. Following
  Bytes from the Minutes page, or Minutes from the Bytes page, keeps the filters, Group by, and the date range, or the
  preset named in the address.
- [ ] `/parent/usage` and the old `/usage` open `/parent/usage/minutes`. The fortnight of bucket tables and the quota
  balance page from S008.4 are gone.
- [ ] Every filter, Group by, and the date range are in the page address. Reload, Back, Forward, a bookmark, and an
  address sent to the other parent open the same graph. Signing in from such an address returns to it, as for every
  portal page in S007.4.

## Filters

The filters sit in the context bar: Client groups, Site groups, Quotas, Group by, Date range, and the preset controls
below. *Changed in implementation:* the title and the preset controls are on the context bar's first line, and the
filters on a second line, which wraps onto a third in a narrow window.

- [ ] Client groups, Site groups, and Quotas each choose any number of the household's groups or quotas, with the
  portal's many-option pull-down. `default` is offered under Client groups and under Site groups.
- [ ] With nothing chosen, a filter does not narrow the graph, and the closed pull-down says All. With no quota chosen,
  traffic that no quota accepts is included. With a quota chosen, only traffic that quota accepted is counted.
- [ ] Group by is Client group, Site group, Quota, or None. Each group draws one line. The groups are those chosen in
  that filter, or every one with usage in the range when nothing is chosen there. None draws one line named Total. For
  example, Client groups younger and older with Group by Client group draws two lines, and Group by Site group with no
  site group chosen draws a line for each site group with usage in the range.
- [ ] Usage by quota is not kept per site group (see the S030 Notes). While a quota is chosen or Group by is Quota, the
  Site groups pull-down is disabled and its title says why. While a site group is chosen or Group by is Site group,
  Quotas is disabled in the same way. Group by offers only the choices the other filters allow. *Added in
  implementation:* an address that asks for both, such as `?site=school&quota=youtube&by=client&range=past-week`,
  leaves out the site groups, or the quotas when it groups by site group, and the status bar says what was left out
  and why.
- [ ] Choosing a group, a quota, Group by, or a date range redraws the graph at once. There is no Apply button.

## Date range

Date range is a single choice: Today, Yesterday, This week, This month, Past week, Past month, Previous week, Previous
month, or Custom. A week runs from Sunday to Saturday. Days and times are household-local.

- [ ] Today runs from local midnight to now. Yesterday is the whole of the previous day.
- [ ] This week runs from the most recent Sunday at midnight to now. This month runs from the 1st at midnight to now.
- [ ] Past week ends now and starts at the same clock time seven days earlier. Past month ends now and starts at the
  same clock time on the same date a month earlier, or on the last day of that month when it is shorter. For example,
  at Friday 13 November 2026 21:30, Past week starts Friday 6 November 21:30, and at 31 March 2027 10:00, Past month
  starts 28 February 2027 10:00.
- [ ] Previous week is Sunday to Saturday of the week before this one, and Previous month is the whole of the calendar
  month before this one. For example, on Friday 13 November 2026, Previous week is Sunday 1 November to Saturday
  7 November, and Previous month is 1 to 31 October.
- [ ] Custom shows From and To date fields, the portal's `DateField`, beside Date range. They expand into view with the
  shared animation. The range covers From through To, whole days. A To before From, or text that is not a date, is a
  problem under the box and the graph is not redrawn until it is fixed. *Added in implementation:* choosing Custom
  fills From and To with the first and last days of the range that was on show, so Past week then Custom on Friday
  13 November 2026 gives 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-13.
- [ ] The status bar says what the range covers and the timezone, such as
  `Friday 6 November 2026 21:30 to Friday 13 November 2026 21:30. Times are Australia/Sydney.`

## Graph

- [ ] The content pane holds a line graph. The horizontal axis is time across the range. The vertical axis is minutes
  on the Minutes page and bytes on the Bytes page, starting at zero.
- [ ] A range of two days or less has one point per hour. A range of up to 92 days has one point per day. A longer range
  has one point per week, Sunday to Saturday. A point at either end of the range that is only partly inside it counts
  only the part inside it, so Past week's first and last days are partial days.
- [ ] On the Minutes page, a point is the length of the buckets that ended in it, as the quota balance counts time. A
  bucket that has not ended yet is not counted.
- [ ] On the Bytes page, a point is the bytes stored in it, including a bucket that has not ended. The axis is labelled in
  KB, MB, GB, or TB, the powers of 1,024 the policy file uses.
- [ ] A line adds every stored row it covers, in minutes and in bytes alike. Usage stored under two site groups, two
  quotas, or two client groups counts once for each. For example, if younger uses YouTube and Classroom in the same
  three-minute bucket, the younger line gains six minutes, and a bucket that both the youtube and screen-time quotas
  accepted counts twice, in minutes and in bytes, on a line that covers both quotas. A line can therefore show more
  than 60 minutes in an hour.
- [ ] Each line has its own colour, and a legend beside the graph names each line with its total for the range, such as
  `younger 6h 20m` or `older 1.4 GB`. More than eight lines reuse the colours with a dashed line.
- [ ] Pointing at a line shows the point's interval, the line's name, and its value, such as
  `Tuesday 10 November 2026, younger, 1h 15m`.
- [ ] A range with no usage shows the empty axes and says `No usage in this range.`
- [ ] *Added in implementation:* each line can be reached with Tab. Focused, the readout starts on its largest point,
  and the left and right arrow keys, Home, and End move it along the line.

## Presets

A preset is a named set of the filters, Group by, and the date range. There is always a preset named Default. The
Preset pull-down and the Save preset and Delete preset buttons sit in the context bar.

- [ ] Minutes or Bytes opened from the sidebar on any other page, or a usage address with neither settings nor a preset
  in it, shows the Default preset. Until one is saved, Default is every client group, site group, and quota, Group by Client group, and
  Past week.
- [ ] Saving the settings on show under the name Default, after the replace question below, makes them what Minutes and
  Bytes open with from the sidebar, in every browser and for both parents.
- [ ] Save preset asks for a name in the portal's name dialog and saves the current settings under it. The new preset is
  then chosen in the Preset pull-down.
- [ ] Choosing a preset puts its name in the page address, such as `/parent/usage/minutes?preset=School%20week`. That
  address, bookmarked or sent to the other parent, opens with the preset's settings as last saved, so replacing the
  preset changes what the address shows. An address that names no saved preset shows Default, and the status bar says
  there is no preset of that name.
- [ ] A relative date range is saved as its name, not as dates. A preset saved with Past week shows the week ending now
  each time it is chosen. A preset saved with Custom keeps its From and To dates.
- [ ] Saving under a name that is already a preset asks whether to replace it, with Cancel as the default. *Added in
  implementation:* names are the same whatever their case, so saving as `school week` asks to replace `School week`,
  and the preset keeps the name it was first saved under. The status bar confirms each save, replace, and delete, such
  as `Saved preset School week.`
- [ ] The Preset pull-down lists every preset by name, in alphabetical order. Choosing one applies all its settings at
  once and redraws the graph.
- [ ] The Preset pull-down shows the preset whose settings match the ones on show, and is blank when none does. Changing
  any filter after choosing a preset puts the settings in the page address in place of the preset's name and blanks the
  pull-down, and changing it back shows the preset again.
- [ ] Delete preset deletes the preset on show, after asking, with Cancel as the default. It is disabled while no preset
  is on show, and while Default is on show, with a title saying Default can be replaced but not deleted.
- [ ] Presets do not hold Minutes or Bytes. The same presets are offered on both pages.
- [ ] Presets are kept by the service. A preset saved in one browser is offered in another, to the other parent's
  account, and after `dw restart`.
- [ ] A preset that names a group or quota no longer in the policy applies the rest of its settings, and the status bar
  names what was left out.

# Notes

## Design hints

- The data endpoint takes the same query string as the page, and returns each line's points already summed, with the
  range and interval it used, so the page only draws. Ranges, intervals, and month and week boundaries are worked out in
  the service in household-local time, where `policy/report.py` and `policy/clock.py` already are.
- A bucket counts in the interval it starts in. Buckets align to local midnight, so with lengths that divide an hour none
  crosses an hour.
- **Charts.** This graph is the first of several: the host bars in S030.2, and later pie or donut charts and vertical
  bars. Build a small shared chart kit in `src/components/charts/` rather than one hand-drawn graph: a frame with axes,
  a legend, and a pointer readout, and the line chart on it. Use `d3-scale` for scales and tick values and `d3-shape`
  for line paths, and later for stacks, arcs, and pies, rendered as SVG by React. Those two cover every chart type
  expected, add only small pure-function packages, and leave the portal in charge of what a general library would
  fight it on:
  - The series colours are role properties, set by class, such as `--chart-series-1` to `--chart-series-8`.
  - Axis labels are household-local times formatted by the service, not in the browser's timezone.
  - Lines and legend entries are DOM elements, so double-click, Enter, focus, and `aria-label` work as everywhere else.

  Recharts 3 is the closest library, SVG and React, but it brings Redux Toolkit, Immer, and react-redux with it.
  ECharts and Chart.js draw on a canvas, which has no focusable elements and needs the theme's colours read back out
  of CSS. visx is the d3 approach wrapped as components, and is a fair alternative if the kit grows beyond these.
- Presets are a new table in `policy.sqlite3`, saved and deleted with JSON POSTs as every portal write is. They hold the
  query string's settings. The built-in Default is not stored until it is first replaced.
- If the controls do not fit the one-line context bar, the filters can take a second line, and
  `docs/parent-portal.md` is updated in the same change.
- When S009 lands, Today, Yesterday, the day points, and the week and month boundaries start at the household day
  boundary rather than at midnight.

## Out of scope

- Drilling into a line. That is S030.2.
- Downloading the raw usage data, or the graph's numbers. A later story.
- Splitting usage by site group and quota together, which needs the site group on quota rows.

## Sizing

21 points, against S007.4 at 21. The parts are the range and interval arithmetic with its calendar edge cases, the
minutes counting across groups, the chart kit and the line graph with its legend and readout, the context bar of
pull-downs with the disabling rules, and presets with their storage, matching, Default, and addresses. It has 35
checks, under the 50 that would make it an
epic, and splitting presets out would leave both halves editing the same context bar and tests.

## Change Summary

The service works out everything the page shows. `usage_graph` reads the address, or the preset it names, fits the
settings to the policy, resolves the range in household-local wall time, splits it into hours, days, or Sunday weeks
clipped at the ends, and sums the ledger rows into one list of values per line. It answers with each point's position
in seconds from the start and its interval as text, the axis ticks as text, the range sentence, the preset whose
settings match, and the notes for the status bar. The page only draws. Quota rows are read whenever a quota is chosen
or the graph groups by quota, and the site-group rows otherwise, so no stored data changes. Two indexes on the start
time keep a long range quick.

The chart kit is SVG drawn by React, with `d3-scale` for the scales and the bytes axis's ticks and `d3-shape` for
the line paths. The minutes axis picks its own steps, such as 15m or 1h, because decimal ticks read badly as
durations. The horizontal axis is a plain linear scale over seconds, never `scaleTime`, so the browser's timezone
never enters. Recharts, ECharts, Chart.js, and visx were not used, for the reasons in the Design hints.

Presets live in `usage_presets`, one row per name with the settings as JSON, and are told apart without regard to
case. Default is built in until it is saved over, and a deleted preset leaves no trace. The page keeps a draft of the
settings so several quick choices in an open pull-down are not lost while the graph for the first is still loading.
Saving and deleting call `api()` from the button with the page on show as the page, so a lapsed session signs in and
returns to the same graph.

## Suggested Acceptance Testing

### Acceptance test cases for S030.1

Rebuild and start with `make up`. The household ledger already has usage. Sign in to the parent pages in two
browsers, one per parent account if there are two.

1. **The sidebar and addresses.** Open Attempts, expand and collapse Usage, reload, then open Minutes and Bytes in turn
   with some filters chosen. Open `/parent/usage` and `/usage`. Copy a usage address into the other browser, and sign
   out in one and reload it. Covers Pages.
2. **Filters and Group by.** On Minutes, choose client groups, site groups, and quotas one at a time, then clear them,
   and try each Group by. Choose a quota and look at Site groups and Group by, then a site group and look at Quotas.
   Open `/parent/usage/minutes?site=school&quota=youtube&by=client&range=past-week` by hand. Covers Filters.
3. **Date ranges.** Note the time, then choose each range and compare the status bar with the examples in Date range.
   Choose Custom, edit From and To, type a To before From, then a word, then fix it. Choose Past week and then Previous
   month to see the point interval change, and a Custom range of a year to see weeks. Covers Date range and the point
   checks under Graph.
4. **Counting.** On a child computer in the younger group, watch YouTube for ten minutes, then open Classroom in
   another tab for a few minutes while the video plays. Open Today on Minutes and on Bytes, before and after the
   current bucket ends. Group by Site group, then Client group, then choose the youtube and screen-time quotas
   together. Point at the lines, and Tab to a line and use the arrow keys. Choose a range before the first stored
   usage. Covers Graph.
5. **Presets.** Save the settings on show as School week, change a filter and change it back, choose Default, choose
   School week again, and bookmark its address. Save again as `school week`, cancel, then replace it with Past month.
   Open the bookmark in the other browser. Save over Default, then open Minutes from the sidebar in both browsers. Run
   `dw restart` and check the presets are still offered. Delete School week, try to delete Default, and open the old
   bookmark. Save a preset with a client group, remove that group from the policy, `dw reload`, and choose the preset.
   Covers Presets.
6. **More than eight lines.** In a household with nine or more site groups that have usage, choose Past month and
   Group by Site group. Otherwise add temporary site groups for sites the child visits, browse each briefly, and remove
   them afterwards. Covers the dashed lines under Graph.
