# User Voice

As a child, I want to practice this week's words and any revision words I missed, by seeing each word and hearing it spoken and spelled out letter by letter, and then start the quiz again from zero.

# Use Cases

## Alphabet audio directory

- Letter audio files live in the task's `alphabet_dir` (`spelling/alphabet` under `DAYWARDEN_DATA_DIR` by default, as configured in S005.1).
- Each file is named for the character it speaks, with any supported audio extension (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.m4a`):
  - `a.<ext>` through `z.<ext>` for the 26 letters (case-insensitive filename matching).
  - `dash.<ext>` for `-` and `apostrophe.<ext>` for `'`.
- `alphabet_dir` must contain one audio file for each letter `a` through `z`, plus `dash.<ext>` if any word in `current` or `revision` contains `-` and `apostrophe.<ext>` if any word in `current` or `revision` contains `'`. Two audio files for the same character in `alphabet_dir` (such as `a.opus` and `a.mp3`) is invalid configuration.

## Starting and leaving practice mode

- A smaller `"Practice"` button sits at the bottom right of both the quiz start screen and the question screen.
- Clicking `"Practice"` opens an in-page confirmation dialog (`"Start practice mode? Your current quiz progress will be reset."` with `"Yes, practice"` and `"No, keep going"`). If a question is showing, its timer keeps running while the confirmation is open, just as for `"Try a different task"`. Enter inside the input box never triggers `"Practice"`.
- Practice mode is a continuation of the current `in_progress` attempt, not an abandonment:
  - It is always allowed, including when the spelling task is the only required task under S003.2. It never marks the attempt `abandoned` and never records a skip day.
  - Confirming `"Yes, practice"` closes any question currently showing as a timeout, resets the quiz progress to zero by starting a new run on the same `in_progress` attempt (preserving any questions and answers already recorded in earlier runs on the attempt), and enters practice mode.
- Practice mode does not finish the attempt as a `pass` and never increments the daily revision count.
- Leaving practice mode early (by reloading or closing the page) and coming back to the task resumes the same `in_progress` attempt on the quiz start screen with both pool scores at zero. Clicking `"Try a different task"` during practice mode follows S003.2 as usual.

## Walking the practice words

- Practice mode walks every entry in `current` (this week's word pool) plus any distinct entries from `revision` that he got wrong (incorrect or timed out, including a question closed as a timeout when entering practice) in the current attempt. The combined list is shuffled into a random order. Other `revision` entries that were not missed in the current attempt are not included.
- For each entry in the practice list:
  - The screen shows the word `<word>` in very large letters in the centre of the screen, along with practice progress at the top (such as `"Practice: 3 of 12"`).
  - A large `"Next"` button (styled with the same blue-on-pale-blue enabled style and pale-grey disabled style as the audio buttons in S005.1, or `"Start quiz"` on the final word) is shown below the word, initially disabled while the audio sequence plays.
  - The browser automatically plays the practice audio sequence for the word, always saying the word both before and after each spelling of it:
    - `word` -> (500 ms) -> `w` (250 ms) `o` (250 ms) `r` (250 ms) `d` -> (500 ms) -> `word` -> (500 ms) -> `w` (250 ms) `o` (250 ms) `r` (250 ms) `d` -> (500 ms) -> `word` -> (500 ms) -> `w` (250 ms) `o` (250 ms) `r` (250 ms) `d` -> (500 ms) -> `word`
    - That is: 250 ms between individual letters during a letter-by-letter spelling, and 500 ms between the spoken word and the letter-by-letter spelling (both from `word` into the first letter and from the last letter back to `word`).
    - Across the sequence, he hears the word 4 times (always immediately before and after each spelling) and hears it spelled out 3 times.
  - When the audio sequence finishes:
    - The prompt `"Say and spell the word until you know it."` appears on screen below the word.
    - The `"Next"` (or `"Start quiz"`) button becomes enabled and focused, so pressing Enter or clicking it moves on.
  - When he clicks `"Next"` to advance to the next practice word, the next word appears and starts its audio sequence.
- Advancing past the last practice word ends practice mode and starts the new scored quiz run on the same attempt with both pool scores at zero.

## Change Summary

- **Alphabet check.** At policy load, and whenever a run scans the pools, `alphabet_dir` must exist and hold exactly one recording of each letter, matched without regard to case. It also needs `dash` and `apostrophe` whenever a word in either pool uses `-` or `'`. A missing character or two recordings of one character fails the load, naming the task, `body.alphabet_dir`, and the character or both files. Hidden files, files that are not audio, and other recordings are ignored. Recording lengths share the existing cache.
- **Practice on the server.** `?do=practice` closes any question showing as a timeout and starts a new run on the same `in_progress` attempt, so both scores are zero and earlier questions stay on the attempt. It never abandons the attempt, records a skip day, or passes the attempt, even when closing the question would otherwise have passed it. It is not subject to S003.2's give-up rules. The words are every current word plus each revision word missed (incorrect or timeout) anywhere in the attempt, once each, shuffled. They are recorded on the new run as `practice`.
- **Sequence.** For each word, the reply lists every clip URL, its length, and the pause before it: the word, 500 ms, the letters 250 ms apart, 500 ms, the word, three times over. The browser only follows the list, so the sequence is tested in Python and the browser's timing is tested in the Node harness.
- **Page.** "Practice" sits at the bottom right of the start and question screens. The confirmation sits above it, and the question timer keeps running while it is open. Like "Try a different task", the Practice buttons never take focus on a click and ignore Enter. Enter in the answer box therefore cannot reach them. Entering practice waits for any request already in flight, so an answer or replay never lands on a closed question. The practice screen shows the word in large letters and "Practice: n of m" at the top. "Next" (or "Start quiz" on the last word) is grey until the sequence ends. Then the prompt appears and the button turns blue and takes the focus, and Enter or a click moves on. "Start quiz" asks the new run's first question. A reload during practice lands on the start screen at zero.

Checked in a browser against a local server with generated clips: the confirmation from both screens with the timer still running, the "1 of 4" count including the revision word that was showing, `don't` and `extemporaneously` on one line, the grey and blue "Next", the focus and prompt when the sequence ends, Enter advancing, the measured pauses between clips, and a reload returning to the start screen at zero.

## Suggested Acceptance Testing

1. With `~/daywarden/spelling/alphabet` recorded, open the spelling task, start the quiz, and click "Practice" on the question screen. Check the confirmation appears with the timer still running, that "No, keep going" returns you to the question with your typing intact, and that "Yes, practice" opens the practice screen.
2. During practice, check each word is shown in large letters with "Practice: n of m" at the top, and that you hear the word, its spelling, and the word again, three times, with short gaps between letters and a slightly longer gap either side of each spelling.
3. Check "Next" is grey while the audio plays and that Enter does nothing then. When the audio ends, check "Say and spell the word until you know it." appears and "Next" turns blue with the focus, and that Enter moves on.
4. Miss a revision word (wrong or timed out), then practise. Check that word is included once, that other revision words are not, and that every current word appears once. Practise from a revision question that is still showing, and check that word is included too.
5. Walk to the last word. Check its button says "Start quiz", and that clicking it starts a quiz at `Current: 0 / n · Revision: 0 / x` that you can pass. In `/attempts/<id>`, check the earlier answers are still there, a new run with `practice` follows, and only the final pass counts towards today's passes.
6. Make the spelling task required (a past `due_date`) and check "Practice" still works and the attempt stays in progress.
7. Reload during practice. Check you land on the start screen at zero on the same attempt. Click "Try a different task" during practice and check it behaves as it does on the quiz.
8. Type a word in the answer box and press Enter. Check it submits and never opens the practice confirmation.
9. Break the alphabet one way at a time: remove `q.opus`, add `a.mp3` beside `a.opus`, or remove `dash.opus` while a word such as `well-known.opus` is in a pool. Restart `decisions` and check each refuses to load with a log line naming the task, `body.alphabet_dir`, and the character or files. Check that removing `dash.opus` with no `-` word still loads.
10. Check a long word such as `extemporaneously` fits on one line of the practice screen at your normal window size.
