# User Voice

As a parent, I want to type in next week's spelling words, record all of them in one go, review the clips, and apply
the list, so that the weekly change takes ten minutes in the browser and last week's words go to revision by
themselves.

# Use Cases

## UC1 - Start the new week

- The spelling page from S023.2 has a "New week's words" button in its context bar. It opens
  `/parent/tasks/<name>/week`, a portal page with its own address.
- A setup in progress is kept on the service. Opening the page again, or reloading it, carries on where the parent
  left off. There is one setup in progress for each spelling task.

## UC2 - Enter the list

- The parent types or pastes the week's words and presses Check. Commas, whitespace, and new lines all separate
  words, and any run of them is one separator, so `rope,,,` then a new line and a space then `cat` is two words.
- Anything that is not a letter is trimmed from the start and end of the whole text, such as the brackets of a list
  copied as `(rope, cat, dog)` from a class posting. A homograph number at the very end, such as `read.1`, is kept.
  Nothing is trimmed from between words.
- A word may be a plain word, such as `read`, or a homograph key, such as `read.1`.
- A word that is not a valid name is refused with the same rule as the pools: letters, `-`, and `'`, with at least one
  letter, up to 22 characters, with an optional `.<n>`. So is a word listed twice, ignoring case.
- The service checks each word against the task's current pool, revision pool, and attic, and reports where it already
  is and whether it already has a word clip and a sentence clip.
- A plain `read` that matches homographs already in the pools, such as `read.1` and `read.2`, is flagged. The parent
  picks which of them belong to the week, or adds a new number.

## UC3 - The week's list

- The week's words are listed in the same layout as the spelling word list: the word, where it is now (New, Current,
  Revision, or Attic), whether it has a word clip, whether it has a sentence clip, and the recording buttons from
  S023.3.
- Clicking a word or sentence that has a clip plays it.
- Each row has a box for a sentence using the word. The text is only a prompt for the speaker. It is kept with the
  setup in progress, survives a reload, and is thrown away on Apply or Cancel. It is never written to a policy or
  data file.
- The parent can edit a word in the list, for example changing `read` to `read.1` after hearing that the existing
  `read` is the other meaning.

## UC4 - A new homograph

- The pools have a plain `read`, and the parent changes the week's `read` to `read.1` for a new meaning. On Apply,
  the new word is `read.1`, and the existing `read` takes the next free number, `read.2`, with its sentence clip renamed
  to match.

## UC5 - Record the whole week in one take

- "Record all" opens a recording dialog with a prompt listing, one line for each clip, every word followed by its
  sentence text. A word with an empty sentence box has only its word line.
- "Record missing" lists only the pieces that have no clip yet: the word line for a word with no word clip, the
  sentence line for a word with no sentence clip and a sentence typed, or both. Words with every piece recorded are
  left out.
- Recording starts on any key and ends on Enter, with nothing to type in between, as in S023.3.
- The service cuts the take, and the clips are assumed to be in the same order as the prompt lines.
- Review uses the S023.3 controls, stepping through prompt lines and clips together. The dialog shows the line
  expected, such as "3 of 18: rope (sentence)", and plays each clip as it comes up:
  - **Keep:** the clip is the expected line. Move to the next line and the next clip.
  - **Junk:** the clip is noise. Discard it and play the next clip, still expecting the same line.
  - **Discard:** the clip is the expected line, or part of it, but it is poor or badly cut. Discard it and move to the
    next line and the next clip.
  - **Missing:** the expected line was not said, was not picked up, or ran into the clip before it. Move to the next
    line, staying on this clip.
  - **Replay** and **Back**, with the same keys, as in S023.3.
- When the clips run out before the lines, the remaining lines stay unrecorded. When the lines run out first, the
  dialog says how many clips were left over.
- When review ends, the list is updated to show which words now have a word clip and a sentence clip.

## UC6 - Apply or cancel

- Kept clips stay pending with the setup. Nothing in the pools changes until Apply.
- Apply stays disabled, saying which words still need a word clip, until every word in the list has one. A missing
  sentence clip does not block Apply.
- Apply, in one step:
  - moves every word now in Current that is not on the week's list into Revision;
  - moves each listed word in Revision or the Attic into Current;
  - writes each new word, and each kept clip for an existing word, into Current, replacing any older clip of that kind;
  - renames an existing homograph as in UC4.
- When the new pools would not load with the task's `current_required` or `revision_required`, Apply is refused with a
  message, as in S023.2, and the page offers to change the target in the same step.
- After Apply, the page goes to the spelling page, and the status bar says how many words became current and how many
  went to revision.
- Cancel asks for confirmation, then throws away the setup, its pending clips, and its sentence text. The pools are
  unchanged.

# Notes

Depends on S023.2 and S023.3.

- The setup in progress, its list, its sentence text, and its pending clips are kept in a hidden directory under the
  data directory, with the pending clips beside a small state file, so Apply is a set of renames on one file system.
- Apply moves files in an order that can be undone. If a move fails partway, the moves already made are reversed, the
  page reports the error, and the setup in progress is kept.
- Apply uses the same move and policy check as a pool change saved on the spelling page in S023.2, and the same
  rewrite of the task's file, hand-edit check, and config history commit when a target is changed.
- A word already current and on the list stays current. Re-recording it here replaces its clip only on Apply.
- The parent session's idle limit applies here as in S023.3. A take refused because the session ended is kept in the
  page for Upload after signing in again.

## Change Summary

New week's words on the spelling page opens `/parent/tasks/spelling/week`. The parent types the week's words, one per
line, and presses Check. Each line is checked with the pools' rule and refused naming the line, and each word is listed
with where it is now, its word and sentence clips, a sentence box, and the three recording buttons. Save or Ctrl+S
keeps the list and the sentences on the service. Record all prompts every word, each followed by its typed sentence,
and Record missing prompts only the pieces with no clip. The review is S023.3's, with the line expected shown as it
comes up. Kept clips wait with the setup, and the pools do not change. Apply is disabled until every word has a word
clip, and then makes the list the current pool in one checked step, through the same path as a spelling page save.
When the targets would refuse the new pools, Apply says why and the Targets box changes them in the same step. Apply
and Cancel return to the spelling page with the outcome in its status bar.

Choices that go beyond, or differ from, the story's notes:

- **The list is edited on the page and saved like any other edit.** Check and Save both send the list, the added lines,
  and the sentences, and nothing is kept until one of them is pressed. While anything is unsaved, Record all, Record
  missing, each row's recording buttons, and Apply are disabled, and say why in their title, so a clip is never kept
  against a word the service has not seen. `docs/parent-portal.md` gains this rule for pages that build up work in
  progress before a final step.
- **A word's sentence and kept clips follow an edit.** Each row tells the service the key it was saved as, so changing
  `read` to `read.1` keeps its sentence and clips. Removing a row with its cross throws away its kept clips when the
  list is saved. A word typed with other letter cases, such as `Rope`, takes the pool's key, `rope`.
- **Picking a homograph.** A new plain `read`, when the pools have numbered meanings of it, lists them with their pools
  and the next free number as checkboxes. Use these changes the row to the first ticked key and adds the others under
  Add words, for the next Check. Apply is refused while any word is unclear.
- **A new plain word beside a numbered one is refused**, such as a new `lead` and `lead.1` on the same list, because
  neither would be in the pools to tell them apart. When the plain word is already in the pools, it is allowed: the
  row says "Becomes lead.2 on Apply", and the row adding the new meaning says "The existing lead becomes lead.2 on
  Apply."
- **An older clip is moved aside, not deleted, until Apply has gone through.** It goes into `.weeks/<task>/replaced/`,
  so every step of Apply is a rename that can be reversed. The setup, with the older clips, is removed once every file
  has moved and the policy file is written.
- **Pasted lists split as they are.** Added after the first delivery, so a list copied from a teacher's email or a
  Google Classroom post needs no tidying. `week.split_words` splits on any run of commas and whitespace, and trims
  anything but letters from the very start and end of the text, keeping a homograph number at the end. Curly
  apostrophes and Unicode hyphens are taken as plain ones. A word must now have at least one letter, so a stray `-`
  or `'` between words is refused rather than listed. A leading apostrophe on the first word, as in `'tis`, is trimmed
  with the rest of the guff.
- **An empty list is no setup.** Saving an empty list removes `.weeks/<task>`, as Cancel does.
- **Apply does not ask about a due date.** A spelling page save warns when a group has already passed a due-dated
  task. Apply changes the words, not whether the task is due, so it does not ask.
- **The Targets box is always on the page.** It shows how many words are current now and the two targets, and a
  changed target is sent only with Apply. After Apply is refused for a target, the box says to change it there.
- The page was checked end to end in Chromium against the real service with a synthetic microphone: the refused lines,
  Check, the homograph choice, reloading, Record missing with Junk, Keep, Discard, and Missing on a real seven-clip
  take, Confirm, a single row's re-record, a refused Apply, lowering `current_required` and applying, the renamed
  `read.2` with its sentence in revision, and Cancel.

## Suggested Acceptance Testing

### Acceptance test cases for S023.4

1. Rebuild and restart with `dw restart` (or `make restart`). Open `/parent/tasks/spelling`. The context bar has a New
   week's words link. Click it: `/parent/tasks/spelling/week` opens, with Tasks › spelling › New week's words in the
   context bar.
2. Under Add words, type three new words, one current word, one revision word, and one attic word, one per line, with
   a blank line and spaces around one word. Press Check. Each word is listed with Now as New, Current, Revision, or
   Attic, and Word clip and Sentence clip as Yes, No, or Play. The status bar counts them.
3. Type a sentence in two of the boxes and press Ctrl+S. Then press Record all. The dialog lists each word on its own
   line, each followed by its sentence, and a word with an empty box has only its word line.
4. Close it, and press Record missing. A current word with a typed sentence and no sentence clip shows only its
   sentence line. A word with a sentence clip and no word clip shows only its word line. Words with every piece
   recorded, and words with no sentence typed and a word clip, are left out.
5. Record a take of the missing pieces: press any key, wait for the recording indicator, press letters and Escape (the
   recording carries on), say each line with a pause, and press Enter. Each clip plays, and the dialog shows the line
   expected, such as `1 of 6: frog (word). Clip 1 of 7.`, with the sentence beneath a sentence line.
6. In that review, make a noise as the first clip and press J on it: the same line is expected and the next clip plays.
   Press D on a poor clip: the next line and the next clip come up, and the summary later shows that line Discarded.
   Skip a line while speaking and press M on the clip after it: the next line is expected with the same clip. Press K
   on a wrong clip, then B: the earlier clip and line come back. R replays.
7. Confirm. The status bar says how many clips were kept, and the list shows "New recording" and "Play new". Click a
   new word and a Play new: each plays the kept clip. `ls ~/daywarden/data/spelling/*` shows no change, and
   `~/daywarden/data/.weeks/spelling/` holds `state.json` and the kept clips.
8. Reload the page, and open it again in a new tab. The list, the sentences, and the kept clips are all still there.
9. While any new word has no word clip, Apply is disabled, and both its title and the line beside Record missing say
   which words still need one. Record a word clip for each with the row's Re-record word button. Apply is enabled,
   even with sentences missing.
10. Before pressing Apply, note `ls ~/daywarden/data/spelling/*`. Press Apply. The spelling page opens and its status
    bar says how many words became current and how many went to revision. `spelling/current` holds exactly the week's
    words, with the new clips as `<word>.opus` and `<word>.sentence.opus`, last week's other current words are in
    `spelling/revision`, the listed revision and attic words are in `spelling/current`, and `.weeks/spelling` is gone.
    The next spelling run on the child's computer asks exactly the week's words as current words.
11. With a plain `read` (and `read.sentence.*`) in the pools, enter `read.1` on the week's list. Its row says "The
    existing read becomes read.2 on Apply." Record it and apply. `spelling/current` has `read.1.opus`, and the old
    recording and its sentence are `read.2.*`, in revision if `read` was current and not on the list.
12. With `read.1` and `read.2` in the pools, enter a plain `read`. Its row asks which belong to this week, listing
    `read.1` and `read.2` with their pools and `read.3 (a new meaning)`. Apply is not ready until one is picked. Tick
    one or more and press Use these, then Save: the row becomes the first ticked key, and the others are on the list.
13. Copy a comma-separated list in brackets from an email or a Google Classroom post, catching the brackets, and paste
    it under Add words. Add a new line and a few words separated by spaces and stray commas. Press Check. Every word is
    listed, the brackets are gone, and a curly apostrophe, as in `don’t`, is listed as `don't`. A list ending in
    `read.1)` keeps `read.1`.
14. Enter `don't`: it is accepted. Enter `rope2`, `cat;dog`, `(cat)` between other words, and a word already on the
    list in another case, such as `Frog`, and press Check. Each is refused in the status bar naming its line, such as
    `Line 2, "rope2": ...`, and nothing is saved. Enter a new plain word and a numbered one of the same word, such as `lead` and `lead.1`, when
    neither is in the pools: it is refused, asking for a number on the plain word too.
15. Set `current_required` in the policy file above the number of words on the week's list, and reload the page.
    Press Apply. It is refused, the status bar names `current_required`, the Targets box says to change it there, and
    the pools are unchanged. Lower Required Correct (Current Pool) in the Targets box and press Apply. It goes through,
    `current_required` in the policy file has the new value, and `git log` in `~/daywarden/config` shows the save by
    the signed-in parent.
16. Press the pencil beside a word and change it, such as `read` to `read.1` after hearing the old `read`, and save.
    Its sentence and any kept clips stay with it. Remove a word with its cross and save: it leaves the list, and its
    kept clips leave `.weeks/spelling`.
17. Re-record word and sentence on one row of the week list, Keep both, and Confirm. The row shows the new clips, and
    the pool files do not change until Apply.
18. With an unsaved change, Record all, Record missing, each row's recording buttons, and Apply are disabled and their
    titles say to save first. Click the spelling link in the breadcrumb, use Back, and Reload: each asks before
    leaving, and Stay keeps the change.
19. Press Cancel. It asks whether to throw away the new week, with Keep it as the default. Escape keeps it. Press
    Cancel again and Throw away: the spelling page opens saying the setup was thrown away, the pools are unchanged,
    and opening New week's words again starts an empty list.
20. Let the parent session expire while recording a long take, then press Enter. The dialog keeps the take and asks you
    to sign in again in another tab. Sign in there, press Upload, and the review carries on.
21. Without a session, `/parent/tasks/spelling/week` goes to sign-in and comes back to that page. The data addresses
    under `/parent/api/tasks/spelling/week` answer 401 and never redirect.
22. Everything in `~/daywarden/data/.weeks` and every clip Apply writes or renames is owned by the host user.
