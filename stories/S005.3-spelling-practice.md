# User Voice

As a child, I want to practice this week's words before the quiz counts, by seeing the word and hearing it spelled, and then start the real quiz from zero.

# Use Cases

- A smaller control at the bottom right of the quiz starts practice mode.
- Starting practice abandons the current quiz attempt and resets both pool scores to zero. Leaving practice, including leaving early, also starts a new quiz attempt at zero.
- Practice walks this week's pool in a random order. It does not use the revision pool.
- For each word, the page shows the word in large letters, plays the word-only audio, then three times plays each letter's audio with about 250 milliseconds between letters and plays the word-only audio again. He has heard the word four times and heard it spelled three times.
- The page then tells him, in text and in audio, to say and spell the word until he knows it. A next control moves to the following word.
- After the last this-week word, practice ends and a new quiz starts with both scores at zero.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Entering practice from a quiz that already had some correct answers leaves those answers on the abandoned attempt and starts the later quiz at zero for both pools.
- [ ] Every this-week word appears exactly once during one practice pass.
- [ ] The word is visible on screen during its practice step. It is not visible during the scored quiz.
- [ ] The audio sequence for a word is word, then three cycles of (letters with a pause of about 250 ms, then the word again). A test can assert the planned sequence without listening.
- [ ] Letter audio is used for each character. An apostrophe or hyphen uses its own clip.
- [ ] The memorization prompt is both on screen and played.
- [ ] Next advances, and the end of the pool returns to a scored quiz with zero scores.
- [ ] Practice does not increment the daily revision count.
