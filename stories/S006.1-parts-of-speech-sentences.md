# User Voice

As a parent, I want to write as many sentences as I like and list which of their words are nouns, verbs, adjectives, or any other part of speech I want him to practise, so that the quiz asks about the grammar he is learning at school.

As a parent, I want the policy load to tell me exactly which sentence and which word I got wrong, so that a typo never becomes a question he cannot answer.

# Use Cases

## UC1 - Write a parts of speech task

- A parts of speech task is a task from S003.1 with `type: parts_of_speech`. Its fields are in the task's `body`.
- The body lists one or more sentences. There is no upper limit. Each sentence has a `parts` table that maps part of speech names to words from that sentence.
- The parent chooses the part of speech names. `noun`, `verb`, `adjective`, `adverb`, `pronoun`, `preposition`, `proper noun`, or `Doing words` are all fine. Each name is a row in the child's table, shown exactly as written, in the order written.
- A sentence's table only shows the parts it lists, so one sentence may ask for nouns and verbs while the next asks for adjectives and adverbs.
- Not every word of the sentence has to be listed. Words left out belong in no row. He should leave them out of the table, and putting one in a row counts as a wrong word (S006.2).
- A part may be given an empty list, such as `adverb: []`. Its row is shown and nothing belongs in it, so it is a row he should leave empty. Each sentence must still have at least one word listed under some part.

```yaml
tasks:
  parts-of-speech-1:
    type: parts_of_speech
    group: younger
    pass_cooldown: 21
    failure_cooldown: 1
    body:
      timeout: 45s
      sentences:
        - sentence: "The quick, brown fox jumped over the lazy dog."
          parts:
            noun: [fox, dog]
            verb: [jumped]
            adjective: [quick, brown, lazy]
        - sentence: "Dry the dishes with a dry towel."
          timeout: 60s
          parts:
            verb: ["dry[1]"]
            noun: [dishes, towel]
            adjective: ["dry[2]"]
```

With two sentences and `correct_answers_required` left out, 2 points are needed to pass (UC4).

## UC2 - Words in a sentence

- A word is a run of letters and digits. An apostrophe (`'` or `’`) or a hyphen (`-`) with a letter or digit on both sides joins the runs either side into one word. `don't`, `o'clock`, `well-known`, and `café` are each one word.
- Everything else is not part of a word: spaces, commas, full stops, quotation marks, dashes, and an apostrophe at the start or end of a run. In `the dogs' bowls`, the word is `dogs` and the apostrophe after it is punctuation.
- He can drag words. Punctuation and spaces are shown in the sentence exactly as written but cannot be dragged (S006.2).
- Words are compared without regard to case, and `’` is the same as `'`. `Dry` at the start of a sentence and `dry` later in it are two occurrences of the same word.

## UC3 - Name a word in the table

- An entry in a part's list is a word from the sentence, optionally followed by one or more occurrence numbers in square brackets: `dry`, `dry[2]`, or `dry[2,3]`.
- Occurrences are counted from 1, in the order the word appears in the sentence, using the comparison in UC2. In "Dry the dishes with a dry towel.", `dry[1]` is `Dry` and `dry[2]` is `dry`.
- An entry without brackets means occurrence 1. `dry` is the same as `dry[1]`.
- `dry[2,3]` puts occurrences 2 and 3 in the same part. Spaces around the word, the brackets, and the numbers are ignored, so `dry[2, 3]` is the same.
- A word that appears `n` times in the sentence can be listed at most `n` times in the whole table, once per occurrence. Each occurrence may be in at most one part.
- In YAML, an entry with brackets or a comma must be quoted inside `[...]`, as in `["dry[1]"]`. A block list (`- dry[1]`) needs no quotes. Unquoted YAML can also turn `yes`, `no`, `on`, `off`, and numbers into something other than text. An entry that is not text is refused with a hint to quote it.

## UC4 - Timer and pass mark

- `timeout` on the body sets the time allowed for each sentence. It defaults to 45 seconds. A sentence may set its own `timeout`, which replaces the body's for that sentence only, so a long sentence can be given longer.
- `correct_answers_required` is the number of points needed to pass. Each sentence is worth up to 1 point (S006.2). It defaults to 70% of the number of sentences, rounded up: 7 for 10 sentences, 4 for 5, 3 for 3, and 1 for 1. The field has the same name as in arithmetic and skip counting.

# Configuration

Body fields:

| Field | Default | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sentences` | Required | A list of one or more sentences, described below. |
| `timeout` | `45s` | The time allowed for each sentence that does not set its own. A duration greater than zero, written as in S004.1, such as `45s` or `1m`. |
| `correct_answers_required` | 70% of the number of sentences, rounded up | The points needed to pass. A whole number from 1 to the number of sentences. |

Sentence fields:

| Field | Default | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sentence` | Required | The sentence text. It must contain at least one word. |
| `parts` | Required | A mapping from part of speech name to a list of entries, as in UC3. At least one part. |
| `timeout` | The body's `timeout` | The time allowed for this sentence. A duration greater than zero. |

A part name is text that is not empty once leading and trailing spaces are removed, and is at most 20 characters long, so the table's left column stays narrow. Two names in one sentence that differ only in case, such as `Noun` and `noun`, are not allowed.

These are invalid configuration. Each one fails the policy load and leaves the previous policy in force. The error names the task, the sentence number, and, where there is one, the part and the entry. For example: `task 'parts-of-speech-1': body.sentences entry 2: parts.adjective: "dry[3]": the sentence has only 2 of "dry"`.

- A missing or empty `sentences` list, a sentence without `sentence` or `parts`, a `parts` with no entries, or a `sentence` with no words in it.
- A sentence whose parts list no words at all, even though each part may be empty.
- An unknown field on the body or on a sentence, or a field of the wrong type. A part whose value is not a list, including a bare `adverb:` with nothing after it, is refused with a hint to write `[]`.
- An entry that is not text.
- An entry whose word part is not a single word by the rules of UC2, such as `fox,` or `ice cream`.
- An entry whose word does not appear in the sentence.
- An occurrence number that is zero, not a whole number, larger than the number of times the word appears, or repeated within one entry, such as `dry[1,1]`.
- Malformed brackets, such as `dry[]`, `dry[1-2]`, `dry[1`, `dry]1[`, or `[1]` with no word.
- One occurrence listed more than once, in the same part or in two parts, including `dry` and `dry[1]` together. The error names every part that lists it.
- A part name that is empty, longer than 20 characters, or the same as another name in that sentence apart from case.
- A `timeout` of zero or a bare number, on the body or on a sentence.
- A `correct_answers_required` of zero, a negative number, a number that is not whole, or a number larger than the number of sentences.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The example task in UC1 loads. The first sentence's words are `The`, `quick`, `brown`, `fox`, `jumped`, `over`, `the`, `lazy`, and `dog`, and the comma and full stop are not words. `correct_answers_required` is 2, the first sentence's timeout is 45 seconds, and the second's is 60.
- [ ] In "Dry the dishes with a dry towel.", `verb: ["dry[1]"]` with `adjective: ["dry[2]"]` puts `Dry` in verb and the second `dry` in adjective. `verb: [dry]` with `adjective: ["dry[2]"]` gives the same result.
- [ ] `verb: [dry]` with `adjective: ["dry[1]"]` is refused, naming verb and adjective. `adjective: ["dry[1,1]"]` and `adjective: ["dry[3]"]` are refused.
- [ ] In "The cat sat on the mat.", `article: ["the[1,2]"]` lists both occurrences of `the`, and `"the[2, 1]"` is the same.
- [ ] `dry[0]`, `dry[]`, `dry[a]`, `dry[1-2]`, `dry[1`, and `[1]` are each refused.
- [ ] An entry is matched without regard to case (`FOX` matches `fox`). `don't` in the table matches `don’t` in the sentence. `well-known` is one word. `fox,` and `ice cream` are refused as not a single word. A word that is not in the sentence is refused.
- [ ] A sentence may leave words out of its table. A part with `[]` is accepted. A sentence whose parts are all empty, an empty `parts`, and a sentence with no words are each refused.
- [ ] A part name that is empty, longer than 20 characters, or a second `Noun` beside `noun`, is refused. Part names keep the order and spelling they were written in.
- [ ] An unquoted `yes` or `3` as an entry, and `adverb:` with no value, are refused with a hint to quote the entry or write `[]`.
- [ ] A body with no `timeout` gives each sentence 45 seconds. A sentence's own `timeout` applies to that sentence only. `0s` and a bare `45` are refused on the body and on a sentence.
- [ ] With `correct_answers_required` left out, 10 sentences need 7 points, 5 need 4, 3 need 3, and 1 needs 1. The 10-sentence case is 7, not 8, so the rounding does not use floating point. An explicit value of 0, 2.5, or more than the number of sentences is refused.
- [ ] An unknown field on the body or on a sentence, a missing or empty `sentences`, and a sentence without `sentence` or `parts` are each refused.
- [ ] Every refusal fails the policy load with the task, the sentence number, the part, and the entry named where there is one, and the previous policy stays in force.
- [ ] Existing tests that use `type: parts_of_speech` as a stand-in task type with an arbitrary body, such as those in `tests/test_task_catalogue.py` and `tests/test_task_selection.py`, are changed to use a valid body or another type, and still pass.
- [ ] `docs/hierarchical-policy.md` and `README.md` describe the parts of speech fields, the word and occurrence rules, the YAML quoting advice, and the defaults.

# Notes

Depends on S003.1 for the task catalogue. S006.2 adds the quiz itself. Until S006.2 is done, a parts of speech task loads and is checked, and the parent should keep it out of the household's policy file or give it a future `available_from`. This story is accepted through the policy file: valid sentences load, and each mistake above fails the load with its message.

Words are kept in sentence order with an occurrence number for each one. S006.2 and S006.3 refer to a word by its position in the sentence, so two occurrences of the same word can be sorted independently.

If S012 is done before this story, tasks are grouped by type in the policy file. The UC1 example is then written as `tasks: parts_of_speech: parts-of-speech-1: ...` with no `type:` field, and errors name the task as `parts_of_speech/parts-of-speech-1`.

Multi-word parts of speech, such as `ice cream` as one noun or `has been` as one verb, are out of scope. The parent lists the words separately or leaves them out.
