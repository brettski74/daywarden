# User Voice

As a parent, I want a parts of speech attempt to show me each sentence, where he put each word on each try, and what the answer was, so that I can see which parts of speech he confuses and talk them through with him.

# Use Cases

## UC1 - Summary

- Opening a parts of speech attempt in the S003.3 review shows a summary above the sentences:
  - his points out of the number of sentences, and the pass mark, such as "6.25 / 10 points, 7 to pass", and whether the run passed, failed, or is still in progress
  - how many sentences were right on the first, second, and third try, how many were wrong after three tries, and how many timed out

## UC2 - Each sentence

- Below the summary, the current run's sentences are listed in the order they were asked. Each one shows the sentence text, the outcome, the try that was right, the points, the time he took, and the time allowed.
- Under the sentence, a table has one row for each part of speech in that sentence. Each row shows the words that belong in it, then, for each try, the words he put in that row.
- A word he put in the wrong row, a word he missed, and a word he put in a row when it belongs in none each stand out from a correct word, by more than colour alone, such as a mark and a short label.
- A try that came from the timer running out is labelled as such.

## UC3 - Runs and other attempts

- An expired run from S004.1 is listed after the current run, under its own heading with the time it expired. It has the same summary and sentence list, and does not count toward the current run's summary.
- An attempt still in progress shows the same view, with the sentences so far. The open sentence shows the tries made so far.
- An attempt of any other type is shown as it was before this story.

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An attempt with 10 sentences, of which 5 were right on the first try, 2 on the second, 1 on the third, 1 was wrong after three tries, and 1 timed out, and 7 points required, shows those counts and "6.25 / 10 points, 7 to pass" with the run failed.
- [ ] Each sentence shows its text, outcome, correct try, points, time taken, and time allowed.
- [ ] Each sentence's table shows, for each part, the words that belong there and the words he put there on each try. In "Dry the dishes with a dry towel.", the two `dry` words are shown in the right rows, and a swap is shown as two wrong words.
- [ ] Wrong, missed, and wrongly sorted extra words are distinguishable from correct ones without relying on colour.
- [ ] A try sent because the timer ran out is labelled.
- [ ] An attempt with an expired run lists the current run first and the expired run after it under its own heading. The expired run's sentences are not counted in the current run's summary.
- [ ] An in-progress attempt shows the summary and the sentences so far, including the open sentence's tries.
- [ ] Arithmetic, spelling, skip counting, and `parent_check` attempts still show as they did before.
- [ ] The view stays behind the parent session from S007.1, like the rest of S003.3.
