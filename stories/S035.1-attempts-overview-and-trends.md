# User Voice

As a parent, I want the attempts page to show how each task went over a period, and each task's points, pass mark,
and questions over time, so that I can see which tasks he is getting better at and which he passes only by grinding
through many questions.

# Behaviour

## Attempts page

- [ ] `/parent/attempts` shows, for the chosen client group, today's summary and the parent check button as before,
      then the overview, then the attempts list.
- [ ] A Period control offers the usage graph's ranges, Today through Custom, with Custom's From and To date fields.
      It starts at Past month. Changing it updates the overview and the list.
- [ ] A Set filter (Required, Potential, Practice) and an Outcome filter (Pass, Failure, Abandoned, Unfinished, In
      progress) start with everything chosen. They apply to the overview, the list, and the trend page. Leaving out
      Practice removes practice attempts from all three.
- [ ] Parent checks are left out of the overview and the trend page. The list still shows the period's parent checks
      as before, whatever the filters.
- [ ] The client group, period, and filters are in the page's address. Reloading, or opening a copied address, shows
      the same view, and choosing another client group keeps the period and filters.
- [ ] The list shows only the attempts in the period that pass the filters, newest first, and each opens as before.
      An attempt from 10 days ago is listed under Past month but not under Past week.

## Attempt figures

An attempt's figures come from its last run: the run that decided its outcome, or the run still in progress. The
overview, the trend, and S035.2 onward use these figures.

- [ ] **Points** and **pass mark**: arithmetic's right answers against `correct_answers_required`, such as 20 / 20.
      Skip counting and parts of speech use their points, such as 6.25 / 7. Spelling has **current points** against
      `current_required` and **revision points** against `revision_required`, such as 8 / 8 and 3 / 4.
- [ ] The pass mark is the one the run started with. After `correct_answers_required` is changed from 10 to 20,
      earlier attempts still show 10 and later ones show 20.
- [ ] **Questions** is the number of finished questions. A question closed by a give-up or a reload counts, as the
      timeout it was stored as. **Maximum** is `maximum_questions` where the run had one: always for parts of speech,
      when it is set for skip counting, and never for arithmetic or spelling.
- [ ] **Wrong** counts questions that ended with no points and did not time out. **Timeouts** counts questions that
      ended in a timeout. An arithmetic run with 20 right, 3 wrong, and 2 timeouts shows Questions 25, Wrong 3, and
      Timeouts 2. A parts of speech sentence right on its second try is neither, and adds 0.5 points.
- [ ] **Time answering** is the sum over the questions of the time from issue to the last answer, or the time allowed
      for a timeout, shown as `m:ss`. **Average per right answer** is the mean of that time over the questions that
      earned points, in seconds to one decimal place. Right in 2.5 s and 3.5 s, then a 4 s timeout, gives 0:10 and
      3.0 s.
- [ ] A run before the last one is left out of the figures. That includes an expired run and a spelling run left for
      practice mode. An arithmetic attempt with an expired run of 12 questions and then a passing run of 22 shows
      Questions 22.
- [ ] An attempt in progress or unfinished has the figures of its questions so far.

## Overview

- [ ] The overview has one row for each task with an attempt in the period that passes the filters, ordered by type
      then name and written `type/name`. Its columns are Attempts, Passes, Failures, Abandoned, Unfinished, Accuracy,
      and Trend. In progress counts only in Attempts.
- [ ] Accuracy is total points divided by total questions over the row's attempts, as a whole percentage. Three
      arithmetic passes of 20 points in 21, 25, and 50 questions show 63%. Spelling adds current and revision points.
- [ ] Trend is a small line of each attempt's points and pass mark, oldest to newest. Spelling's line adds current
      and revision points, and its pass mark adds both targets. A screen reader hears the number of attempts and the
      latest points and pass mark, such as "6 attempts, latest 20 of 20".
- [ ] A task no longer in the policy is still listed, marked "not in the policy". A renamed task is listed once,
      under its new name, with all its attempts.
- [ ] With nothing to show, the overview says "No attempts in this period."
- [ ] A task's name opens its trend page with the same client group, period, and filters.

## Task trend

- [ ] `/parent/attempts/tasks/<type>/<name>` is headed `type/name`, has the same Period, Set, and Outcome controls in
      its address, and has a link back to `/parent/attempts` that keeps them.
- [ ] A counts chart has one marker for each attempt, placed at its start time across the period, with lines joining
      the attempts in order.
- [ ] The counts chart offers Points, Pass mark, Questions, Maximum, Wrong, and Timeouts. Spelling offers Current
      points, Current required, Revision points, and Revision required in place of Points and Pass mark. Maximum is
      offered only when an attempt in the period had one. *Changed in implementation:* Points, Pass mark, and
      Questions start shown. For spelling, its four and Questions start shown.
- [ ] Each line is shown or hidden from its legend entry, and the lines shown are in the address.
- [ ] Each marker's shape tells its outcome: pass, failure, abandoned, unfinished, or in progress. A practice attempt's
      marker is hollow. A legend explains the shapes, so nothing relies on colour alone.
- [ ] A time chart under the counts chart, on the same horizontal axis, offers Time answering and Average per right
      answer, both shown to start with, and hidden and shown as on the counts chart. *Changed in implementation:*
      Time answering reads against the left axis in `m:ss`, and Average per right answer is a dashed line against its
      own axis on the right, in seconds, so both are readable though one is minutes and the other seconds.
- [ ] Pointing at a marker, or focusing it, shows a readout with the attempt's start date and time, set, outcome, and
      every figure. The left and right arrow keys move between attempts. Clicking a marker, or Enter on it, opens that
      attempt's page.
- [ ] Under the charts, the task's attempts in the period that pass the filters are listed as on `/parent/attempts`.
- [ ] With no attempts to show, the page says, for example, "No attempts at arithmetic/sums for younger in this
      period." That includes a type and name that is not a task and has no attempts.
- [ ] Opening a trend address while signed out asks to sign in, then returns to that address.

# Notes

Depends on S003.3 for the attempts page, S012 for task identity, and S030.1 for the Period control, its ranges, and
the chart components. Figures are worked out from each attempt's stored `detail` when the page is asked for. No new
table is needed for a household's volume of attempts. A type added later needs its own figures before its tasks show
anything but counts.

The existing `LineChart` draws series with focus and keyboard movement, but has no outcome markers, no hollow
markers, no way to open one point, and no lines toggled from the legend. The trend chart needs all four. The usage
graph should keep working as it does. Sparklines are small inline SVGs with no interaction.

Time is the time from issue, as stored: `taken_ms` for arithmetic, and the last try's `received_ms` for the other
types. Spelling's allowed time includes its audio.

Points, Pass mark, and Questions start shown because together they show both whether he passed and how hard it was.

## Change Summary

Figures are worked out from each attempt's stored `detail` when a page is asked for, as the Notes say. The last run is
the last entry of `detail.runs`, and only the questions stored with its run number count. The pass mark comes from the
settings stored on the attempt when that run started: `detail.settings` for arithmetic, skip counting, and parts of
speech, and the run's own `current_required` and `revision_required` for spelling. Skip counting can leave
`correct_answers_required` out, so its pass mark is worked out by the type's own settings parser. Every timeout counts
the time allowed, including an arithmetic answer that came in late, which is stored with its real time.

A range that ends now, such as Past month, also takes in an attempt whose start is a moment after now. Without this,
an attempt from a clock that just stepped back, or a test that plants one slightly ahead, would vanish from the list.

`set` and `outcome` are left out of the address while every choice is made, and `set=none` stands for no choice. The
trend's lines are in `show`, left out while they are the defaults. Showing or hiding a line changes only `show`, and
the trend page's `shouldRevalidate` skips asking for its data again.

Each attempt's markers on a trend chart sit in one SVG `<a href>` to its attempt page, so middle click and Copy link
address work. A plain click or Enter opens it in the portal without a reload. Only the newest attempt is in the Tab
order at first, and the arrow keys, Home, and End move focus between attempts. `LineChart` is untouched, so the usage
graph works as before.

Checked in Chromium against a scratch service seeded with a month of attempts: layout, the shared time axis, hollow
practice markers, the hover and focus readouts, the arrow keys, and Enter opening the attempt.

## Suggested Acceptance Testing

### Acceptance test cases for S035.1

Rebuild and start with `make up`, and sign in to the parent pages. The checks need a client group with a few weeks of
attempts at arithmetic, skip counting with `maximum_questions` set, parts of speech, and spelling, including a practice
attempt, an abandonment, one left unfinished from an earlier day, and a parent check.

1. **Attempts page and period.** Open `/parent/attempts` and choose the group. Check today's summary and Record parent
   check are as before, then the overview, then the list. Change Period through Past week, Custom with From and To, and
   back, and check the overview and list follow, including an attempt about 10 days old. Reload, copy the address into
   another tab, and choose another client group. Covers the Attempts page area.
2. **Filters.** Turn Practice off in Set, and outcomes off in Outcome, and check the overview, the list, and a task's
   trend all lose them, while the period's parent checks stay listed. Covers the Set and Outcome and parent check items.
3. **Overview figures.** Compare a row's counts against the list, and work out Accuracy from the attempt pages' answers.
   Check the sparkline, and with a screen reader its "N attempts, latest X of Y". Remove a task from the policy and
   rename another, and check the "not in the policy" mark and the single renamed row. Choose a period with nothing in
   it. Covers the Overview area.
4. **Attempt figures.** Do an arithmetic attempt with known right, wrong, and timed-out answers, a reload during a
   question, and one that expires after an hour idle before the run that passes. Change `correct_answers_required`
   between two attempts. Check the trend's readouts. Repeat with a parts of speech sentence right on its second try,
   and a spelling attempt that goes into practice mode first. Covers the Attempt figures area.
5. **Trend charts.** Open a task's trend from the overview. Check the heading, the back link, the offered and
   starting lines for arithmetic, spelling, and a capped skip counting task, hiding and showing from the legend and
   reloading, the marker shapes and hollow practice markers against the key, and the time chart's two axes. Point at
   and Tab to a marker, use the arrow keys, then Enter, and click and middle-click another. Covers the Task trend
   chart items.
6. **Trend edges.** Open `/parent/attempts/tasks/arithmetic/nothing?client=<group>` and a task with nothing in the
   period. Sign out and open a trend address, sign in, and check it comes back. Covers the empty and signed-out items.
